North Gujarat Castor Belt: Banaskantha Sourcing Advantage

Gujarat produces more than 70% of India's total castor crop, and the arid-to-semi-arid belt of North Gujarat (centered in Banaskantha and Vav-Tharad) is recognized globally as the premier agricultural zone for high-yield, high-oil-content castor seed (Ricinus communis).

1. Agronomic Sourcing Superiority: Banaskantha & Vav-Tharad

The unique microclimate of North Gujarat—characterized by deep sandy-loam alluvial soils, low annual rainfall (300–500 mm), intense solar radiation, and cold, dry winter maturation cycles—is ideal for the castor plant:

  • Superior Oil Content: Seeds harvested in Banaskantha consistently achieve 48% to 52% natural oil content, significantly higher than seed cultivated in humid tropical or southern agricultural zones (typically 42%–46%).
  • High Ricinoleic Acid Concentration: The stable temperature during capsule formation ensures a ricinoleic fatty acid fraction of 88% to 90% in crude oil, minimizing non-hydroxy fatty acid impurities.
  • Low Free Fatty Acid (FFA) at Harvest: Dry harvest conditions prevent seed mold, pod fermentation, and enzyme activation, resulting in farm-gate seed FFA levels typically below 1.0%.

2. Strategic Proximity to Major Castor APMC Mandis

Nagdev Products' manufacturing facility in Sardarpura (Taluka Deodar) is situated at the epicenter of India's most liquid agricultural produce market committees (APMC mandis):

Deodar APMC

Immediate local market; direct daily intake of fresh farmgate seed.

Tharad & Vav APMC

Primary high-volume trading hubs for northern border castor farmers.

Bhabhar APMC

Key regional transit mandi connecting Vav-Tharad to transport arteries.

Palanpur & Dhanera APMC

District financial centers anchoring year-round seed buffer reserves.

3. Integrated Value Chain vs. Distant Converters

Many international buyers purchase derivatives from secondary processors located thousands of kilometers away from the seed crushing zone. Distant processors rely on trans-shipped crude oil that has suffered oxidative exposure and acidity build-up during rail and tanker transit.

By contrast, Nagdev Products represents an integrated farm-to-chemical facility:

  • Fresh local seed crushed daily in our 50 MT/day facility.
  • Expelled oil transferred directly to continuous neutralization, bleaching, and hydrogenation reactors without intermediate storage oxidation.
  • By-product de-oiled cake returned directly to local Banaskantha farmers as high-nitrogen organic fertilizer, completing a sustainable circular economy.

Frequently Asked Buyer Questions

When does the castor harvest season occur in North Gujarat? +

The primary harvest begins in November and peaks between January and April, though year-round mandi arrivals continue thanks to extensive farm and warehouse storage networks.

What is Nagdev Products' crushing capacity? +

The facility is engineered with an integrated seed crushing capacity of 50 MT/day alongside continuous chemical refining and downstream derivative reactors.
