Export Logistics & Packing

Typical Export Packaging & Container Loading Guide

Standardized, export-grade packaging configurations engineered for international containerized shipping.

Liquids & Waxes
200L MS / HDPE Drums

Epoxy-coated Mild Steel or high-density polyethylene drums with tamper-evident seals for refined oils and liquid derivatives.

Net Weight / Drum:200 kg / 225 kg
20' FCL Capacity:80 Drums (16.0 – 18.0 MT)
Palletization:4 drums/pallet, shrink-wrapped
Bulk Liquids
1,000L IBC Totes

Rigid composite intermediate bulk containers with galvanized steel cage and integrated bottom discharge valve.

Net Weight / Tote:950 kg – 1,000 kg
20' FCL Capacity:18 – 20 IBCs (17.1 – 19.0 MT)
Discharge Valve:2" / 3" Camlock / Flanged
Flakes, Powders & Pastilles
25 kg Multi-Ply Bags

Heavy-duty multi-layer kraft paper bags with inner polyethylene moisture-barrier liner for HCO flakes, 12-HSA, and metal soaps.

Net Weight / Bag:25.0 kg
20' FCL Capacity:640 – 720 Bags (16.0 – 18.0 MT)
Palletization:Fumigated wooden/plastic pallets
High Volume Bulk
ISO Tanks & Flexitanks

Direct bulk liquid container loading for high-volume industrial clients with steam-heating pad options for cold-climate delivery.

Flexitank (20' FCL):20.0 – 22.0 MT
ISO Tank Container:21.0 – 24.0 MT
Heating Option:Steam / Hot water coils

Loading quantities are indicative and subject to product density, packaging configuration, container payload restrictions and carrier requirements. Please confirm with our logistics team before booking.
