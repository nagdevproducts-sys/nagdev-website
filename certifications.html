Quality Standards & Certifications Roadmap

At Nagdev Products, our core corporate philosophy is built on absolute integrity and technical accuracy. We operate strictly under quality management systems aligned to ISO 9001 and provide transparent, verifiable status updates regarding all international quality certifications.

Our Corporate Commitment to Honesty:

Nagdev Products does not claim unearned or premature certifications. Our manufacturing facility is engineered and constructed strictly in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 and compendial GMP standards. Formal external audit certifications are scheduled to coincide with commercial plant commissioning in 2027. Below is the honest, auditable status of each standard.

How Quality Is Guaranteed for 2027 Consignments

Even prior to formal institutional audit seals, every consignment produced and shipped by Nagdev Products is governed by enforceable contractual safeguards:

  • Batch-Specific Certificate of Analysis (COA): Tested in our in-house QC laboratory against compendial ASTM, AOCS, BIS, and BP/USP test standards.
  • Independent Third-Party Pre-Shipment Inspection: Buyers are authorized to appoint accredited inspection bodies (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek) to draw independent samples from production batches at the plant prior to container stuffing.
  • Lot Traceability: Complete end-to-end trace from farm-gate seed intake and APMC mandi lot to reactor batch and container seal number.
