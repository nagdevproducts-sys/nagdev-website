About Castor Derivatives

The Versatility of Castor.

Castor oil is one of nature's most functional bio-based materials. Derived from the castor bean (Ricinus communis), its high content of ricinoleic acid gives castor and its derivatives unique physical and chemical properties that make them useful across a broad range of industrial applications.

Nagdev Products manufactures and exports a comprehensive range of castor oils and derivatives — from refined castor oil and hydrogenated castor oil to specialty chemicals including fatty acids, esters, waxes and metal soaps — for use in industries ranging from lubricants to cosmetics, coatings to agriculture.

Industries & Applications

Key Market Segments.

Castor oil and derivatives serve these industrial application areas.

🔧
Lubricants
Castor oil is a natural bio-lubricant with excellent viscosity-temperature characteristics. It is widely used as a base oil or additive in industrial lubricants and specialty lubrication applications.
Relevant Products:
Commercial Castor Oil HCO / Castor Wax 12-HSA
⚙️
Lubricating Greases
12-Hydroxystearic acid (12-HSA) and its metal soaps (lithium, calcium) are key thickener components in lubricating grease manufacture. Widely used in lithium complex and calcium-based greases.
Relevant Products:
12-HSA Lithium 12-Hydroxystearate Calcium 12-Hydroxystearate
🎨
Paints, Coatings & Inks
Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO) is a fast-drying diene oil used in alkyd resins, paints and varnishes. Ricinoleic acid is used in alkyd resin synthesis and printing inks.
Relevant Products:
Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO) DCO Fatty Acid Ricinoleic Acid
🧱
Plastics & PVC
Sebacic acid esters (DOS — Dioctyl Sebacate, DBS — Dibutyl Sebacate) are widely used as plasticisers for PVC and synthetic rubber, particularly for low-temperature flexible applications.
Relevant Products:
Dioctyl Sebacate (DOS) Dibutyl Sebacate (DBS) Sebacic Acid
🔄
Rubber & Elastomers
Castor oil is used as a processing aid and softener in rubber compounding. Zinc ricinoleate is used as a zinc activator in rubber vulcanisation and processing.
Relevant Products:
Castor Oil Zinc Ricinoleate
🧴
Personal Care
Ricinoleic acid and its derivatives are used as emollients, emulsifiers and functional ingredients in skin care, hair care and personal hygiene products.
Relevant Products:
Ricinoleic Acid Methyl Ricinoleate Refined Glycerin
🧴
Cosmetics & Pharma
Zinc undecylenate (antifungal active), zinc ricinoleate (deodorant active), FSG refined castor oil and pure refined glycerin for cosmetic and pharmaceutical formulations.
Relevant Products:
Zinc Undecylenate Zinc Ricinoleate Refined Glycerin FSG Castor Oil
🏗️
Polyurethane
Castor oil and castor-based polyols are used as bio-based polyol components in polyurethane foam systems, coatings and elastomers, reducing dependence on petroleum-derived polyols.
Relevant Products:
Castor Oil Bio-PU Polyols
🌾
Agriculture
Castor cake — the by-product of castor oil extraction — is a high-nitrogen organic fertilizer used in agriculture. It also has pesticidal properties and is suitable for organic farming.
Relevant Products:
Castor Cake Fertilizer
Why Castor

The Natural Advantage of Castor.

Bio-Based

Castor oil is a 100% bio-based, renewable raw material derived from castor beans — not petroleum.

Unique Chemistry

High ricinoleic acid content (>85%) gives castor unique functional properties not found in most other natural oils.

Versatile

The functional hydroxyl group in ricinoleic acid enables a wide range of chemical modifications — from simple refining to complex specialty derivatives.
