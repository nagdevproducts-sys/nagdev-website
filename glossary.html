Analytical Nomenclature & Quality Metrics

Castor Oleochemistry Technical Glossary

An authoritative guide to key chemical parameters, ASTM/AOCS testing standards, and molecular metrics governing refined castor oils, hydrogenated waxes, and fatty acid derivatives.

Buyer Guides → Explore Product Catalog
AOCS Cd 13-60 / ASTM D1957 mg KOH/g
Hydroxyl Value (OHV)

The number of milligrams of potassium hydroxide (KOH) equivalent to the hydroxyl content of one gram of chemical substance. In castor oil, it quantifies the ricinoleic acid concentration.

Read Full Definition & Method →
AOCS Cd 1d-92 (Wijs Method) g I₂/100g
Iodine Value (IV)

The mass of iodine in grams consumed by 100 grams of chemical substance, measuring the degree of unsaturation (carbon-carbon double bonds).

Read Full Definition & Method →
AOCS Cd 3d-63 / ISO 660 mg KOH/g
Acid Value (AV) & Free Fatty Acids (FFA)

The number of milligrams of potassium hydroxide (KOH) consumed by the free fatty acids present in one gram of fat or oil.

Read Full Definition & Method →
AOCS Cd 3-25 mg KOH/g
Saponification Value (SAP)

The number of milligrams of potassium hydroxide (KOH) required to saponify (hydrolyze into glycerol and soap) one gram of fat or oil.

Read Full Definition & Method →
Gas Chromatography (GC-FID) / AOCS Ce 1e-91 % by weight
Ricinoleic Acid Content

The primary fatty acid constituent of castor oil: 12-hydroxy-cis-9-octadecenoic acid (C18H34O3), representing 85% to 92% of the oil's total fatty acid profile.

Read Full Definition & Method →
ASTM D1544 (Gardner) / AOCS Cc 13b-45 (Lovibond 1" or 5¼" Cell) Gardner Units (1–18) / Lovibond (Y + R)
Gardner Colour & Lovibond Colour Scale

Visual color measurement standards for liquid resins, oils, and chemical derivatives, measuring yellow, red, and amber hues.

Read Full Definition & Method →
Process Reaction / Monitored via Iodine Value (AOCS Cd 1d-92) Reaction Process
Catalytic Hydrogenation

A chemical reaction where molecular hydrogen (H2) is added across the carbon-carbon double bonds of castor oil in the presence of a nickel catalyst, transforming liquid oil into hard crystalline wax.

Read Full Definition & Method →
AOCS Ca 2c-25 / Karl Fischer ASTM E1064 % by weight / ppm
Moisture, Volatile Matter & Impurities (M&I)

The percentage of water and insoluble suspended matter present in castor oil, determined via oven drying or Karl Fischer coulometric titration.
