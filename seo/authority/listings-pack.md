## 3. LinkedIn Company Profile Description (96 words, ≤ 120 words)

Nagdev Products Private Limited is a Gujarat-based castor seed crushing and castor oil refining manufacturer located in Deodar, District Vav-Tharad. The company operates a 50 TPD mechanical seed crushing mill and multi-stage refining lines (refining, bleaching, and filtration) producing commercial castor oil, refined First Special Grade (FSG) castor oil, first pressed degummed oil, hydrogenated castor oil, dehydrated castor oil, DCO fatty acid, and expeller castor cake. Operating from North Gujarat's agricultural castor belt, Nagdev Products provides domestic and export supply in drums, IBCs, and bulk ISO tanks with batch-specific Certificate of Analysis documentation.
