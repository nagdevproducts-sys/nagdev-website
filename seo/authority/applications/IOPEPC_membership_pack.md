# IOPEPC (Indian Oilseeds and Produce Export Promotion Council) Membership & RCMC Application Pack

## 1. Official Portal & Membership URLs
- **Official Website:** https://www.iopepc.org
- **Membership & RCMC Portal:** https://www.iopepc.org/membership / DGFT e-RCMC Portal (https://www.dgft.gov.in)
- **Council Scope:** Designating export promotion council for vegetable oils, castor oil, and oilseed-derived oleochemicals in India.

## 2. Eligibility Rules & Membership Fees
- **Eligibility:**
  - Registered manufacturing/exporting entity in India.
  - Valid Import Export Code (IEC) issued by DGFT.
  - Active GST registration.
- **Membership Categories:**
  - Manufacturer Exporter (Nagdev Products Private Limited qualifies as a Manufacturer Exporter with an operational seed crushing and oil processing plant).
  - Merchant Exporter.
- **Fee Structure (Official IOPEPC Schedule):**
  - **Entrance Fee (One-time):** ₹5,000 + 18% GST (approx. ₹5,900)
  - **Annual Membership Subscription (Based on Export Turnover / New Member tier):**
    - New Member / Turnover up to ₹1 Crore: ₹7,000 + 18% GST (approx. ₹8,260)
  - **RCMC Issuance Fee:** Included with membership subscription or nominal DGFT portal fee (₹1,000 + GST).
  - *Reference URL:* https://www.iopepc.org/membership/fees-structure
