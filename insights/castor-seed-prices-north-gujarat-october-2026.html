North Gujarat Castor Seed APMC Prices & Mandi Arrivals: October 2026

Publisher: Nagdev Products Private Limited Published: 26 October 2026 Updated: 26 October 2026 Coverage Area: Deesa, Palanpur, Tharad, Patan, North Gujarat

This market report compiles official wholesale agricultural market committee (APMC) spot price data and arrival volumes for castor seed (Ricinus communis) across the primary commercial mandi yards of North Gujarat for the four completed weeks of October 2026. The documented mandis include Deesa, Palanpur, Tharad, and Patan, which collectively serve as the principal seed procurement basins across North Gujarat's castor belt (Banaskantha and Vav-Tharad region).

Wholesale prices in Gujarat agricultural markets are reported in Rupees per quintal (1 quintal = 100 kg) on an as-arrived basis, with moisture and foreign matter subject to standard APMC fair average quality (FAQ) lot evaluation. All spot transaction data presented herein is sourced directly from daily market returns compiled by the Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (DMI), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India through the Agmarknet Portal, alongside monthly crop monitoring bulletins published by The Solvent Extractors' Association of India (SEA).

Market Structure and Regional Price Dynamics

During the four reporting weeks of October 2026, wholesale castor seed modal prices across North Gujarat trading yards hovered within a steady range between ₹8,528 and ₹8,678 per quintal. Price differentials between northern procurement centers such as Tharad and central hubs such as Deesa remained narrow, typically reflecting local transport differentials to processing clusters rather than structural shifts in physical seed availability.

Arrival volumes across the four monitored APMC yards reflected normal seasonal movement for late kharif harvesting cycles. Deesa maintained the highest individual weekly intake, averaging 1,420 metric tonnes, supported by direct farm deliveries from surrounding agricultural blocks. Tharad recorded consistent weekly turnover averaging 1,180 metric tonnes, drawing arrivals from agricultural producers across the Vav, Tharad, and border belts. Weekly modal prices reached their monthly apex during Week 3 at ₹8,678 per quintal in Palanpur and ₹8,657 per quintal in Siddhpur before leveling off toward ₹8,600 to ₹8,640 per quintal in Week 4.

Crushing mills in North Gujarat calculate raw material parity based on direct seed oil content (averaging 47% to 49% in certified regional seed lots) and expeller cake yield. Spot seed transactions in these regulated yards supply local mechanical expeller operations, which subsequently route crude castor oil to chemical refining and hydrogenation facilities. For industrial oleochemical procurement managers, spot mandi movements in Deesa, Palanpur, and Tharad provide the baseline input valuation for basic commercial oil, First Special Grade (FSG), and derivative fatty acids.

Methodology Notes

  • Data Collection: Price and arrival figures are drawn from official daily market bulletins registered on the Agmarknet database (agmarknet.gov.in) maintained by the Ministry of Agriculture.
  • Pricing Metrics: Modal price represents the statistical mode of executed auction lots for FAQ (Fair Average Quality) grade seed during each calendar week. Minimum and maximum extremes resulting from damaged or exceptional moisture lots have been excluded to represent commercial trading parity.
  • Geographic Scope: Market yards selected represent the core agricultural catchment area directly feeding crushing infrastructure in North Gujarat.
  • Scope Limitation: This report provides historical observational market data only. It contains no price forecasts, investment recommendations, or commercial purchasing advice.
