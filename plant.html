Manufacturing Facility & Processing Infrastructure

Integrated castor oil expelling, chemical refining, catalytic hydrogenation and derivative production in North Gujarat's castor belt (Banaskantha–Vav-Tharad).

Operational Plant · Supplying Commercial Volumes
🏗️
Plant Status: Integrated facility operational in Banaskantha — mechanical seed pressing, semi-continuous oil refining, and batch pressure reactors active. Regional seed sourcing and commercial dispatches active. Note: Facility visuals displayed include architectural and operational representations.
Facility Architecture

Engineered for Integrated Value Addition.

Our manufacturing facility in North Gujarat's castor belt (Banaskantha–Vav-Tharad) is operational and engineered for end-to-end castor processing — encompassing mechanical seed crushing, alkali refining, catalytic hydrogenation, and specialty chemical synthesis.

Every production stream is configured with dedicated QA/QC testing protocols matching strict ASTM, AOCS, and Indian Standard (IS) specifications before batch release.

Manufacturing Process.

Key stages of our castor oil manufacturing and derivative production process.

01
Raw Material Handling

Castor seed sourced from North Gujarat's castor belt (Banaskantha–Vav-Tharad). Incoming raw material assessed for quality before processing.

Representative image
02
Oil Extraction & Processing

Mechanical extraction and processing of castor seed (seed crushing capacity: 50 TPD) to produce crude and commercial grade castor oil.

03
Refining

Degumming, neutralisation, bleaching and deodorising to produce refined grades including First Special Grade (FSG) castor oil.

04
Derivative Production

Chemical processing including hydrogenation, esterification and saponification to produce specialty derivatives.

Representative image
05
Quality Control

Laboratory testing of key analytical parameters. Products released against defined technical specifications only.

Representative image
06
Packing & Dispatch

Multiple packing options — steel drums, HDPE drums, IBC totes, multi-ply bags and bulk — for domestic and export requirements.
