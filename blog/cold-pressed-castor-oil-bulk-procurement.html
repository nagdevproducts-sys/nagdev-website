What is Cold Pressed Castor Oil?

Cold Pressed Castor Oil, widely termed virgin castor oil, represents the pure, unheated primary extraction fluid recovered from the seeds of Ricinus communis. Unlike conventional commercial expelling—which utilizes thermal seed conditioning and high-friction multiple pressings to maximize oil extraction yield—cold pressing relies strictly on a single, gentle mechanical pass where operating temperatures are kept below 50°C. No steam cooking, external heating coils, or chemical extraction solvents are employed during the extraction cycle.

Industrial buyers and cosmetic formulators prioritize cold pressed castor oil bulk procurement because the gentle extraction preserves the native triglyceride structure, natural tocopherols, and bio-active constituents without thermal degradation or oxidation. The oil exhibits low initial peroxide values, minimal free fatty acid content, and an exceptionally light, fresh aroma. Nagdev Products produces authentic cold pressed castor oil at our specialized manufacturing facility in Deodar, North Gujarat, supplying verified bulk packaging for personal care formulators, specialty chemical manufacturers, and commercial export buyers.

How Nagdev Produces Cold Pressed Castor Oil in Deodar

Nagdev Products manufactures Cold Pressed Castor Oil at our plant located at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330. Integrated with our 50 TPD seed crushing capacity, our production protocol enforces strict mechanical processing controls:

  1. Seed Cleaning and Destoning: High-grade whole castor seeds sourced from North Gujarat agricultural mandis pass through rotary seed cleaners, de-stoners, and magnetic grids to remove dust, stones, and vegetable debris.
  2. Single-Pass Cold Screw Pressing: Cleaned whole seeds feed directly into dedicated mechanical screw presses operating at low rotational speeds. Mechanical friction is carefully managed using water-cooled expeller shafts to ensure internal oil temperatures never exceed 50°C.
  3. Gravity Settling of Crude Oil: The freshly expelled virgin oil flows into stainless steel settling tanks, allowing coarse seed particles to settle naturally under gravity without chemical flocculants.
  4. Precision Plate-and-Frame Filtration: The settled oil passes through multi-layer filter presses fitted with virgin cotton filter cloths, removing particulate fines down to complete optical clarity.
  5. Quality Verification and Packaging: The clarified cold pressed oil transfers into clean, dedicated storage tanks where laboratory technicians verify acidity, peroxide value, and moisture before packaging.

Technical Specifications & Plain Parameter Explanations

Quality control managers evaluate Cold Pressed Castor Oil against strict analytical limits:

  • Free Fatty Acids (FFA): Specified at 1.00% maximum (calculated as oleic acid). Low acidity verifies that the seeds were fresh and pressed without thermal breakdown.
  • Peroxide Value: Capped below 5.0 meq O2/kg at dispatch. Low peroxide value confirms that the cold pressing process avoided thermal oxidation, ensuring long shelf life and oxidative stability.
  • Moisture and Volatile Matter: Controlled below 0.25% maximum. Minimal moisture prevents biological spoilage and eliminates cloudiness during storage.
  • Hydroxyl Value: Maintained between 160 and 168 mg KOH/g, confirming full retention of natural ricinoleic acid triglyceride functionality.
  • Colour (Lovibond Scale): Measured in a 1-inch cell, specified below 15 units (Yellow + 5xRed). The natural pale golden-yellow appearance reflects gentle mechanical processing without chemical bleaching.
  • Refractive Index (at 40°C): Specified between 1.4700 and 1.4740, providing rapid spectroscopic verification of botanical authenticity.

Primary Applications of Cold Pressed Castor Oil

The purity and gentle extraction profile of Cold Pressed Castor Oil make it a versatile raw material across technical and cosmetic industries:

1. Cosmetic and Personal Care Formulations

Cold pressed castor oil is utilized in natural lipsticks, lip glosses, mascara, and foundation emulsions. Its high viscosity provides long-lasting gloss, pigment wetting, and smooth sensory spreadability.

2. High-Purity Hair Care and Conditioning Serums

In selected hair oils, conditioning balms, and scalp treatments, cold pressed oil acts as an emollient base that coats hair shafts, delivering natural luster without synthetic silicones.

3. Specialty Technical Lubricants and Precision Instruments

Because of its low acidity and absence of thermal degradation products, cold pressed oil serves as a specialty lubricant for precision machinery, clockwork mechanisms, and food-processing equipment where natural botanical vegetable lubricants are required.

4. Natural Soaps and Artisanal Emollients

In cold-process soap manufacturing, cold pressed castor oil is reacted with sodium hydroxide to create rich, stable lather and provide moisturizing properties in selected specialty bar soaps.

Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What extraction methodology defines cold pressed castor oil? +

Cold pressed castor oil is produced exclusively via single mechanical screw pressing of cleaned castor seeds without external thermal heating, pre-cooking, or chemical solvents, maintaining processing temperatures below 50°C.

How does cold pressed oil differ from standard commercial castor oil? +

Standard commercial oil utilizes multi-pass expelling with thermal conditioning, whereas cold pressed oil captures only the virgin, unheated primary extraction fluid, preserving natural antioxidants and minimizing peroxide formation.

What technical specifications govern cold pressed castor oil? +

Specifications include free fatty acids below 1.0%, moisture under 0.25%, peroxide value below 5.0 meq O2/kg, and a pale yellow visual clarity with hydroxyl value of 160 to 168 mg KOH/g.
