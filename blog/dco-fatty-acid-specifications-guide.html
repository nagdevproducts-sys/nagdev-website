What is DCO Fatty Acid (Dehydrated Castor Oil Fatty Acid)?

Dehydrated Castor Oil Fatty Acid (DCO-FA) is a specialized distilled oleochemical intermediate derived from Dehydrated Castor Oil. While whole DCO is a triglyceride ester containing three fatty acid chains bound to a glycerol backbone, DCO-FA represents the free, unesterified fatty acid mixture recovered after high-pressure splitting (hydrolysis) followed by fractional vacuum distillation. Chemically, DCO-FA consists primarily of conjugated 9,11-octadecadienoic acid (approximately 30% to 35%) and non-conjugated 9,12-octadecadienoic acid (approximately 45% to 55%), alongside minor quantities of saturated fatty acids.

Resin chemists and polymer engineers rely on this DCO fatty acid specifications guide because DCO-FA represents one of the most reactive and versatile bio-based conjugated fatty acids available to the chemical industry. Because it contains free carboxylic acid functionality (-COOH) and elevated conjugated unsaturation without glycerol, DCO-FA can be reacted directly with specialized polyols (such as pentaerythritol, trimethylolpropane, and neopentyl glycol) to engineer high-solids baking enamels, epoxy esters, and clear-drying alkyds with pronounced hardness and chemical resistance. Nagdev Products produces high-purity DCO Fatty Acid at our Deodar processing plant in North Gujarat.

How Nagdev Produces DCO Fatty Acid in Deodar

Nagdev Products manufactures DCO Fatty Acid at our manufacturing facility located at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330. Integrated with our 50 TPD seed crushing capacity, our production chain executes high-efficiency splitting and vacuum distillation:

  1. DCO Precursor Dehydration: High-purity refined castor oil is thermally dehydrated under high vacuum in the presence of acidic catalysts to form the conjugated octadecadienoic triglyceride backbone.
  2. High-Pressure Hydrolysis (Splitting): The dehydrated oil is charged into a continuous high-pressure splitting column where counter-current demineralized steam splits the triglyceride molecules into crude free fatty acids and an aqueous glycerin co-product stream.
  3. Phase Separation & Sweetwater Recovery: The aqueous glycerin sweetwater is drawn off for separation, while the crude DCO fatty acids pass to a drying column to eliminate residual moisture.
  4. High-Vacuum Fractional Distillation: The crude fatty acid stream enters high-vacuum distillation towers operating under deep vacuum. Light boiling fractions and heavy pitch residues are separated, distilling pure, clear DCO Fatty Acid overhead.
  5. Polishing & Nitrogen-Blanketed Storage: The distilled DCO-FA is cooled, filtered, and transferred into dedicated stainless steel storage tanks under continuous dry nitrogen blanketing to prevent atmospheric oxidation.

DCO Fatty Acid Specifications Guide: Synthesis & Quality

This DCO fatty acid specifications guide outlines the technical advantages of direct polyol formulation. By utilizing distilled fatty acids rather than whole triglycerides, alkyd manufacturers eliminate the high-temperature alcoholysis (glycerolysis) step, reducing batch cooking cycle times by up to 30%. Furthermore, formulators can precisely tailor the polyol blend to achieve high cross-link density, lower VOC content, and enhanced resistance to chemical attack. Technical resin formulators rely on this DCO fatty acid specifications guide to calibrate their stoichiometric calculations.

Technical Specifications & Plain Parameter Explanations

Industrial formulators evaluate DCO Fatty Acid against strict analytical parameters documented on the Certificate of Analysis:

  • Acid Value: Specified between 195.0 and 204.0 mg KOH/g. The high acid value confirms complete hydrolysis and provides the stoichiometric baseline for calculating polyol charges during polyester and alkyd cooking.
  • Saponification Value: Specified between 198.0 and 206.0 mg KOH/g, demonstrating complete fatty acid conversion and minimal residual neutral oil.
  • Iodine Value (Wijs): Specified between 140.0 and 155.0 g I2/100g. The elevated iodine value quantifies the total double bond concentration, verifying rapid oxidative drying performance.
  • Conjugated Diene Content: Maintained between 30% and 35% minimum (measured by UV spectrophotometry). Conjugated dienes provide rapid free-radical polymerization during air-drying and stoving bake cycles.
  • Colour (Gardner Scale): Maximum 3 to 4 Gardner. The pale liquid appearance ensures that resins cooked with DCO-FA do not impart unwanted darkness to clear finishes or pastel architectural coatings.
  • Titer (°C): Below 5.0°C. Low titer ensures that DCO-FA remains a clear, pumpable liquid at standard operating temperatures without crystallizing in transfer lines.

Primary Industrial Applications of DCO Fatty Acid

The conjugated fatty acid architecture of DCO-FA supports heavy-duty coating and resin manufacturing:

1. High-Solids and Stoving Alkyd Enamels

DCO-FA is the ideal fatty acid for producing short-oil and medium-oil baking alkyds cured with melamine-formaldehyde or urea resins. These stoving enamels provide enhanced hardness, chemical resistance, and color-stable performance on automotive components, home appliances, and metal furniture.

2. heavy-duty Epoxy Esters

Reacting DCO-FA with bisphenol-A epoxy resins produces flexible epoxy esters used in heavy-duty anti-corrosive maintenance primers, offshore structural coatings, and marine barrier primers.

3. Printing Ink Resins and Overprint Varnishes

In lithographic and web-offset printing inks, DCO-FA is cooked into modified phenolic and hydrocarbon resins to provide fast drying speeds, sharp dot resolution, and high gloss on coated paper stocks.

4. Air-Drying Polyurethane Modified Resins

DCO-FA is incorporated into urethane-modified alkyds (uralkyds) to manufacture tough, abrasion-resistant clear wood varnishes, gym floor coatings, and marine topside enamels.

Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DCO Fatty Acid and how does it differ from whole DCO? +

DCO Fatty Acid (DCO-FA) is the distilled free fatty acid mixture obtained by hydrolyzing Dehydrated Castor Oil and removing glycerin. It consists predominantly of conjugated and isolated octadecadienoic acids with an acid value near 200 mg KOH/g.

Why do resin formulators select DCO-FA over whole triglyceride DCO? +

DCO-FA allows direct esterification with polyols like pentaerythritol and trimethylolpropane without requiring an initial alcoholysis (transesterification) stage, giving resin formulators precise control over polymer architecture.

What are the core technical specifications for DCO Fatty Acid? +

Typical specifications include an acid value of 195 to 204 mg KOH/g, soap formation value of 198 to 206 mg KOH/g, iodine value of 140 to 155 g I2/100g, and titer below 5°C.

What bulk packaging is used for DCO-FA shipments? +

DCO-FA is supplied in 190 kg tight-head steel or HDPE drums, 950 kg composite IBC totes, and dedicated stainless steel road tankers or ISO tanks.

Can international buyers request third-party pre-shipment inspection? +

Yes. Export enquiries welcome. Independent inspection agencies such as SGS or Intertek can be appointed to verify batch parameters prior to dispatch.
