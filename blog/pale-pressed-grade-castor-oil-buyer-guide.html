What is Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) Castor Oil?

Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) castor oil represents the absolute zenith of optical purity and refining excellence among industrial-grade castor oils. Derived exclusively from the initial, low-temperature mechanical screw pressing of selected, selected-grade Ricinus communis seeds, PPG captures the pure virgin triglyceride fluid with minimal extraction of dark natural pigments, mucilage, or hydrolytic degradation products. The expelled oil subsequently undergoes dedicated chemical alkali refining, washing, vacuum dehydration, and intensive bleaching with high-activity adsorbent clays.

Chemical engineers and procurement specialists rely on this pale pressed grade castor oil buyer guide when selecting raw materials for applications that cannot tolerate background color, acidity, or residual moisture. While standard First Special Grade (FSG) is acceptable for general industrial formulations, PPG is specifically demanded in water-white polyurethane resins, clear electronic potting compounds, and light-stable specialty polymers. How Nagdev Produces Pale Pressed Grade Castor Oil in Deodar

Nagdev Products manufactures Pale Pressed Grade castor oil at our plant located at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330. Integrated with our 50 TPD seed crushing capacity, our production protocol for PPG enforces rigorous raw material selection and gentle processing:

  1. Seed Selection and Triple Screening: Only hand-selected, freshly harvested hybrid castor seeds from North Gujarat mandis are chosen for PPG production. The seeds pass through triple-deck vibratory screens, air separators, and magnetic grids to ensure zero foreign matter.
  2. Gentle First Expeller Pressing: The seeds are pressed in mechanical screw expellers under reduced barrel pressure and controlled low temperature. This captures the lightest, purest triglyceride fraction while leaving secondary oil in the cake.
  3. Precision alkali refining: The crude pale oil is treated with a precise stoichiometric volume of dilute sanitary sodium hydroxide. This selectively precipitates free fatty acids into insoluble soaps without hydrolyzing triglycerides, reducing FFA below 0.8%.
  4. Multi-Stage Demineralized Water Washing: The refined oil is thoroughly washed with hot, demineralized soft water to remove trace soap and polar impurities down to undetectable levels.
  5. High-Vacuum Dehydration: The washed oil enters a high-vacuum flash drying column, removing entrained moisture below 0.20%.
  6. Double Bleaching & Polishing Filtration: The dehydrated oil is treated with specialized acid-activated bleaching earth and activated carbon under vacuum, followed by multi-pass filtration through closed polishing presses, producing sparkling, water-pale PPG oil.

Technical Specifications & Plain Parameter Explanations

Quality control managers evaluate Pale Pressed Grade castor oil against tight analytical limits:

  • Colour (Lovibond Scale): Specified at 1.5 units maximum (Yellow + 5xRed in a 1-inch cell). This pale clarity ensures that formulations remain crystal clear without yellow tinting.
  • Free Fatty Acids (FFA): Capped below 0.80% maximum (calculated as oleic acid). Ultra-low acidity prevents catalyst deactivation in urethane reactions and avoids interference during sensitive esterifications.
  • Moisture and Volatile Matter: Controlled below 0.20% maximum. Extremely low water content eliminates moisture-induced bubbling during polyurethane casting and prevents hydrolytic degradation during extended storage.
  • Hydroxyl Value: Maintained between 160 and 168 mg KOH/g, verifying that the secondary hydroxyl group at carbon 12 is fully preserved and available for cross-linking reactions.
  • Specific Gravity (at 15°C/20°C): Measured between 0.958 and 0.968 g/cm³, reflecting standard high molecular density.
  • Refractive Index (at 40°C): Specified between 1.4700 and 1.4740, confirming botanical purity and absence of foreign oil adulteration.

Primary Industrial Applications of Pale Pressed Grade Oil

The extreme optical clarity and low acidity of PPG castor oil make it integral in heavy-duty sectors:

1. Optical Polyurethanes and Electronic Potting

PPG is the preferred bio-polyol for formulating transparent polyurethane potting compounds used to encapsulate light-emitting diodes (LEDs), sensitive electronic circuit boards, and solar photovoltaic junction boxes. Its low moisture and optical clarity prevent clouding and electrical short circuits.

2. High-Clarity Sealants and Structural Adhesives

In architectural glass sealants, moisture-cure joint fillers, and transparent polyurethane adhesives, PPG reacts smoothly with isocyanates to form tough elastomeric bonds that resist UV degradation and maintain transparency.

3. Pale Specialty Alkyd Resins and Finishes

Resin manufacturers cooking water-white architectural lacquers, clear furniture varnishes, and pastel baking enamels use PPG to achieve clean pastel shades without yellow undertones.

4. Dielectric Insulating Fluids

Because of its low moisture content and low acidity, PPG is utilized in specialized high-voltage electrical transformers, capacitors, and switchgear as a biodegradable, fire-resistant dielectric insulating fluid.
