Oleochemistry Glossary & Analytical Testing

Iodine Value (IV)

Iodine Value (IV) measures the total degree of carbon-carbon unsaturation (double bonds) present in fatty acid chains. In oleochemical processing, measuring IV tracks catalytic hydrogenation (converting liquid oil to wax) or thermal dehydration (synthesizing conjugated drying oils for coatings).

Standard Method AOCS Cd 1d-92 / ISO 3961 (Wijs Method)
Reporting Unit g I₂/100g
Typical Range 82 – 90 g I₂/100g (Castor Oil) · < 3.0 g I₂/100g (Castor Wax / HCO)

Chemical Principle & Wijs Titration

The Wijs method exposes the sample to an iodine monochloride (ICl) glacial acetic acid solution. Halogen atoms add stoichiometrically across olefinic double bonds. The unreacted halogen is reduced with potassium iodide, and liberated elemental iodine is titrated against standardized sodium thiosulfate using a starch indicator.

Monitoring Catalytic Conversions in Castor Processing

  • Hydrogenation Completeness: Virgin castor oil has an IV of 82–90. In our hydrogenation reactors, hydrogen gas is added across the C9=C10 double bond. An IV dropping below 3.0 confirms complete conversion to hard Hydrogenated Castor Wax (HCO) with a melting point of 85–88°C.
  • Dehydration (DCO) Synthesis: Dehydrating ricinoleic acid eliminates the hydroxyl group to generate a second double bond. The resulting Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO) exhibits an elevated IV of 130–145 g I₂/100g, creating rapid-drying conjugated alkyds for industrial protective coatings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if Hydrogenated Castor Oil has an Iodine Value above 5.0? +

An IV above 5.0 indicates incomplete hydrogenation. The material will have a depressed melting point, increased tackiness, and impaired rheological performance in coatings and PVC lubricants.

Is castor oil classified as a drying or non-drying oil? +

With an Iodine Value of 82–90, natural castor oil is classified as a non-drying oil. It does not air-oxidize or form films at ambient temperatures, making it an exceptional base for lubricants and hydraulic fluids.
