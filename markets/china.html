International Trade & Regulatory Guide

Castor Oil Supply to China: Trade & Port Logistics

China is a primary global importer of Indian castor oil for conversion into nylon-11, and industrial polyurethanes. Here is an overview of import regulations, quality standards, and ocean freight logistics from Mundra and Kandla.

Request a Quote → ← Global Export Hub

1. Chinese Market Overview & Processing Landscape

China is a major individual importer of Indian castor oil by volume, importing hundreds of thousands of metric tons annually to feed its massive domestic chemical sector. Chinese downstream processors convert Indian castor oil into sebacic acid, nylon-11, polyurethane foams, and industrial lubricants.

Key commercial grades demanded across Chinese coastal ports:

  • First Special Grade (FSG) & Commercial Castor Oil: Large-volume feedstock for catalytic cracking, alkali fusion to sebacic acid, and polyurethane prepolymers.
  • Castor Wax (HCO): Consumed in heavy industrial coatings, PVC compounding, and masterbatch processing.
  • 12-HSA (under development): Widely utilized in China's automotive grease manufacturing hubs across Jiangsu, Shandong, and Guangdong.

2. Chinese Customs & Documentation Framework

  • General Administration of Customs of China (GACC): Import declarations require full matching of commodity HS tariff codes (1515.30 for castor oil), manufacturer plant details, and clean quality inspection certificates.
  • Inspection & Quarantine: Consignments must be free of extraneous matter. Inspection certificates from Indian export authorities accompany expeller meal shipments.
  • Key Gateway Ports: Direct container vessel connections from Mundra/Kandla serve Shanghai, Ningbo, Qingdao, and Huangpu in approximately 14 to 20 days.
