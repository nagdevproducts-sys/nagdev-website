Product Availability

Core Castor Products Available for Bulk Procurement

Our integrated crushing plant in Deodar, Gujarat produces, refines, and stocks 7 primary commercial grades under strict in-house laboratory controls:

First Special Grade (FSG) →

Bleached, refined, low-moisture flagship grade (FFA < 1.0%, Lovibond (5¼"): Max 20Y + 2R). Supplied in flexitanks, ISO tanks, and drums for polyurethanes, lubricants, and chemical synthesis.

Bulk ISO Tank · Flexitank · Drum
Commercial Castor Oil →

First expeller-pressed raw industrial castor oil (FFA < 2.0%, Hydroxyl 160–168). Engineered for large-scale industrial splitting, alkyd resin cooking, and lubricant additive formulations.

Road Tankers · Flexitanks · Drums
Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO) →

Fully hydrogenated hard vegetable wax (Melting Point 85°–88°C, Iodine Value: ≤ 4.0). Supplied in uniform flakes or powder for lithium greases, powder coatings, wax polishes, and PVC lubricants.

25 kg Paper Bags · 1,000 kg Pallets

Hydrolyzed pure hydroxy fatty acid (Acid Value 175–182, MP 74°–77°C). The industry benchmark thickener for high-temperature multi-purpose lithium and lithium-complex lubricating greases.

Transaction Certainty

Structured B2B Procurement Workflow

We maintain a systematic commercial execution procedure to give global purchasing teams complete clarity across contract negotiation, quality verification, and dispatch:

01
Specification Alignment

Buyer submits required product grade, targeted chemical parameters (FFA, Lovibond, IV, AV), required packaging format, monthly/annual tonnage, and destination port.

02
Sample & COA Evaluation

Nagdev Products dispatches a 500g to 1 kg technical evaluation specimen alongside lot-specific laboratory Certificates of Analysis (COA) for bench validation.

03
Commercial Contract & Pricing

Execution of Proforma Invoice / Supply Agreement detailing Incoterms 2020 (FOB Kandla/Mundra, CFR, CIF), container allocation schedule, and payment security (LC at sight / CAD / advance TT).

04
Processing & Independent Inspection

Production and packaging executed at our Deodar plant. Optional third-party sampling and testing by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek executed at factory or port terminal at buyer's cost.

05
Port Gate-in & Bill of Lading

Direct container transport to Kandla Port (~220 km) or Mundra Port (~260 km). Full shipping document set (Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, COA, SDS) surrendered promptly.

Enterprise Compliance

COA Standards, Documentation & Third-Party Auditing

To satisfy strict corporate compliance requirements, every consignment leaving our Gujarat manufacturing plant is backed by exhaustive technical documentation:

  • Batch-Wise Certificate of Analysis (COA): Verified in-house data documenting exact analytical values against contract limits (moisture, FFA, color, iodine value, hydroxyl value, melting point). Learn how to interpret analytical parameters in our guide: How to Read a Castor Oil COA.
  • Technical Data Sheets (TDS): Comprehensive physical and chemical property profiles, application notes, and handling guidelines for each product grade.
  • Safety Data Sheets (SDS / MSDS): Fully GHS-compliant 16-section safety data sheets covering ecological profile, storage compatibility, toxicology, and emergency measures.
  • Customs & Trade Documentation: Certificate of Origin (COO), Commercial Invoices with exact HS classification codes (Castor Products HS Codes Guide), phytosanitary certificates, and packing declarations.

Corporate buyers wishing to review plant operational certifications can visit our Certifications & Standards Hub or examine our corporate infrastructure profile on About Nagdev Products.

Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Bulk Castor Oil Procurement

What are the packaging options for bulk castor oil orders?

For domestic deliveries in India, our standard order packaging is standard full truckload quantities (tankers, drums, or palletized bags). For overseas export orders, our standard Order volume is one 20-foot Full Container Load (FCL), which accommodates 80 steel drums (16 MT), 18–20 composite IBC totes (16.2–19.0 MT), or one 21.5 MT flexitank.

Which bulk packaging formats are available for liquid castor oils and solid derivatives?

Liquid products (FSG, Commercial, Ricinoleic Acid, Refined Glycerin) are packaged in 200 kg new steel drums, 1,000 L composite IBC totes, 21.5 MT flexitanks, and 20–24 MT ISO tank containers. Solid derivatives (Hydrogenated Castor Oil wax and 12-HSA) are supplied in 25 kg moisture-barrier multi-wall paper bags with PE liners, palletized and stretch-wrapped (20 MT per 20ft container).

Does Nagdev Products provide lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA) with bulk dispatches?

Yes. Every commercial shipment leaves our plant with a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA) issued by our internal laboratory, validating acid value, iodine value, hydroxyl value, moisture content, melting point, and color against contracted specifications.

Can international buyers arrange pre-shipment quality inspection by SGS or Bureau Veritas?

Yes. We welcome third-party pre-shipment sampling, testing, and container sealing by accredited independent agencies such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek at the buyer's cost prior to dispatch from our Gujarat facility or port terminal.

What are the standard export ports and delivery Incoterms offered?

Our plant in Deodar connects directly to Kandla Port (~220 km) and Mundra Port (~260 km). We support FOB Kandla/Mundra, CFR destination port, and CIF destination ports with complete maritime export documentation.
