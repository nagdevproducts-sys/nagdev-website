Oleochemical Architecture

The Unique Ricinoleic Chemistry Behind Castor Derivatives

Unlike conventional commodity seed oils (such as palm, soy, or sunflower) which consist primarily of saturated and unsubstituted unsaturated triglycerides, natural castor oil (CAS 8001-79-4) features an robust fatty acid distribution comprising 85% to 90% ricinoleic acid (cis-12-hydroxyoctadec-9-enoic acid, C18H34O3).

The presence of an active secondary hydroxyl group (-OH) at the 12th carbon, combined with a cis-double bond between carbons 9 and 10, grants castor oil natural hydroxyl functionality (hydroxyl value 160–168 mg KOH/g), high oxidative stability, and high kinematic viscosity. Through controlled chemical transformations—including catalytic hydrogenation, basic alkaline hydrolysis, dehydration, pyrolysis, and transesterification—Nagdev Products converts raw North Gujarat seed oil into high-purity industrial intermediates used across global manufacturing.

Hydroxyl Functionality

Enables direct reaction with diisocyanates to form non-petrochemical polyurethane polyols, polyols for coatings, and structural bio-resins without secondary chemical functionalization.

High Melting Wax Solids

Full catalytic saturation of the double bond yields Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO), a hard, brittle vegetable wax with a sharp melting point of 85°C to 88°C and complete water insolubility.

Grease Gelling Precursor

Under development, hydrolysis of HCO yields 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA), the world standard thickener for lubricating greases, reacting with lithium hydroxide to produce high drop-point soap fibers.

Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions on Castor Oil Derivatives

What are castor oil derivatives?

Castor oil derivatives are bio-based oleochemicals manufactured by modifying the unique ricinoleic triglyceride molecule naturally found in castor seed. Because castor oil contains a natural secondary hydroxyl group at carbon-12 and a monounsaturated double bond, it can be industrially transformed through hydrogenation, fat splitting, dehydration, and pyrolysis into functional chemicals like HCO wax, 12-HSA, and ricinoleic acid, as well as downstream materials like sebacic acid and bio-polyols currently under development.

Which castor oil derivatives are currently in regular commercial supply from Nagdev Products?

Nagdev Products maintains steady, operational production and dispatch for 7 core grades: Commercial Castor Oil, First Special Grade (FSG) Castor Oil, First Pressed Degummed (FPD) Castor Oil, Expeller Castor Cake, Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO), Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO), and DCO Fatty Acid.

What is the primary industrial application of 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA)?

Under development at Nagdev Products, 12-Hydroxystearic Acid is the primary gelling and thickening agent used across the lubricants industry to produce heavy-duty lithium-based and lithium-complex lubricating greases. It imparts enhanced drop points, mechanical stability, water resistance, and shear endurance under high automotive and industrial operating temperatures.

What packaging options are available for bulk castor oil derivatives?

Solid derivatives (such as regular HCO wax and 12-HSA under development) are supplied in 25 kg multi-wall craft paper bags with an inner polyethylene liner, palletized and stretch-wrapped. Liquid grades are packaged in 200 kg new steel drums, 1,000 L composite IBC containers, or 20–24 MT bulk ISO tanks and flexitanks.

Can international buyers request independent third-party quality inspection?

Yes. Every production batch is accompanied by our internal factory Certificate of Analysis (COA). For overseas export contracts, buyers may appoint recognized third-party surveyor agencies (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) to conduct independent sampling, testing, and container sealing at buyer's cost prior to port dispatch.
