Strategic Origin & Company

Direct Sourcing from India's Castor Belt.
Engineered for Industrial Demand.

Nagdev Products Private Limited operates an industrial crushing plant in North Gujarat's castor belt (Banaskantha and Vav-Tharad region). Direct proximity to source provides consistent farm-gate access to seeds with high natural oil content.

From mechanical expeller crushing to refining and filtration, our integrated plant supplies technical oleochemical specifications to lubricant, grease, polyurethane, and coating manufacturers across domestic and export markets.

Buyer Questions & Verification

Frequently Asked Questions.
Commercial & Technical Clarifications.

Direct, transparent answers regarding our manufacturing capacity, product portfolio, commercial timeline, and procurement procedures.

Who is Nagdev Products? +

Nagdev Products Private Limited is an integrated Indian castor oil and chemical derivatives manufacturer situated in North Gujarat's castor belt, operating a mechanical expeller seed crushing facility and advanced processing units for technical castor derivatives.

Where is the plant located? +

Our manufacturing facility is located at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330, India, with direct arterial highway connectivity to Kandla and Mundra ports.

Which castor oil grades do you offer? +

We offer 27 industrial grades across our portfolio: 7 Regular Supply grades (Commercial Castor Oil, First Special Grade FSG, Castor Cake, Hydrogenated Castor Oil, Ricinoleic Acid, 12-Hydroxystearic Acid, and Refined Glycerin), 7 On Enquiry grades (Cold Pressed, BP 93, PPG, FPD, High Protein Castor Meal, Crude Glycerin, and Sodium Sulphate Anhydrous), and 13 Under Development specialty derivatives (including Sebacic Acid, DCO, DCO Fatty Acid, Bio-PU Polyols, DOS, DBS, methyl esters, and metallic soaps).

Is your plant operational? +

Yes. Our integrated plant in North Gujarat is operational, with 50 TPD seed pressing, semi-continuous oil refining and batch reactors for derivatives.

Do you export? +

We supply domestic buyers and accept export enquiries, with export documentation available for each shipment: COA, SDS, commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin; third-party inspection (SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek) arranged on request at buyer's cost.

How can I request a sample or quote? +

You can submit your technical specifications, required volume, and destination on our Contact page, email us directly at nagdevproducts@gmail.com, or message our commercial sales team on WhatsApp at +91 63520 98306.
