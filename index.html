Strategic Origin & Company

Direct Sourcing from India's Castor Belt.
Engineered for Global Industry.

Nagdev Products Private Limited is strategically located in Banaskantha, Gujarat — the agricultural epicenter of global castor seed production. Direct proximity to source provides consistent farm-gate access to seeds with superior natural oil content (48–52%) and high ricinoleic purity.

From primary expelling and refining to catalytic hydrogenation and complex specialty derivatives, our integrated operations supply high-reliability oleochemical solutions to lubricant, grease, polyurethane, and coating manufacturers across worldwide markets.
