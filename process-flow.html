Primary Processing · Mechanical Seed Pressing

Castor Seed Pressing & Oil Mill Flow

From certified seed receiving and QC sampling through automated screen cleaning to high-pressure mechanical expelling, yielding unrefined Commercial Castor Oil and expeller castor cake (typically 8–10% residual oil) for organic soil conditioning.

ENGINEERING SCHEMATIC: MECHANICAL SEED PRESSING & OIL RECOVERY
Unit Process Intermediate / Byproduct Commercial Output

📋 Stage-by-Stage Engineering Breakdown

STAGE 01 Intake & Storage

Seed receiving & QC sampling

Direct farm-gate seed consignments from North Gujarat undergo gross and tare verification and QC sampling. Quality inspectors draw composite core samples for rapid lab analysis of seed moisture, foreign matter, and seed purity.

Seed Quality:Selected Hybrid Seed
Seed Moisture:Max. 6.0%
STAGE 02 Zero Waste

Multi-Deck Cleaning & Byproduct Handling

Vibratory screens, rare-earth permanent magnetic drums, and de-stoners remove stems, stones, and iron tramp metal. Light husks and pods are aspirated and conveyed directly to covered storage facilities.

Cleaning Efficiency:> 99.5%
Husks Destination:Covered Storage
STAGE 03 Expelling

Continuous High-Torque Screw Expelling

Cleaned seeds are conditioned and crushed in heavy-duty mechanical screw expellers . Approximately 40% of the seed mass is extracted as unrefined commercial crude castor oil, while the remaining approximately 60% forms expeller cake containing typically 8–10% residual oil.

Mechanical Oil Yield:Approximately 40% of Seed Mass
Cake Residual Oil:Typically 8–10%
View Commercial Castor Oil Specs →
STAGE 04 Cake Processing

Expeller Cake Conditioning & Bagging

Hot expeller cake discharged from the press barrels is gently cooled and conditioned into consistent flakes. Containing ~8–10% residual oil and typical 4.0–4.5% organic nitrogen, this cake serves as an organic slow-release fertilizer and soil conditioner.

Cake Nitrogen (N):4.0% – 4.5% (typical)
Residual Oil:8.0% – 10.0% (typical)

Secondary Processing · Adsorption & Bleaching

Castor Oil Chemical Refining Flow

Advanced degumming, foots sedimentation, high-vacuum thermal moisture stripping, Activated Bleaching Earth & Carbon adsorption, and hermetic vertical pressure leaf filtration producing FSG/BSS, PPG, and British Pharmacopoeia (Castor Oil BP) grades.

ENGINEERING SCHEMATIC: THERMAL ADSORPTION REFINING
Unit Process Filter Aid (Hyflow) Bleaching Earth (Tonsil) Certified Product

📋 Refining Operation & Quality Parameters

STAGE 01 Recycle Loop

Decantation & Foots Recycle

Crude oil settles in continuous conical decanters where heavy mucilaginous gums and solid meal fines gravimetrically separate. Clarified supernatant moves forward to bleaching, while settled foots are continuously recycled back to the expellers for closed-loop recycled oil recovery.

Foots Separation:Gravimetric Settling
Oil Loss:0.0% (Recycled)
STAGE 02 Triple Dosing

Precision Adsorption Chemical Dosing

Clarified oil is treated in an automated slurry dosing reactor with Hyflow filter aid (0.1%–0.3%) for leaf pre-coat, Tonsil / Galleon acid-activated bleaching earth (1.0%–1.5%) for chlorophyll/carotene removal, and Activated Carbon (0.75%–1.0%) for deep polyaromatic odor stripping.

Bleaching Earth:1.0% – 1.5% Tonsil
Activated Carbon:0.75% – 1.0%
STAGE 03 Thermal Vacuum

High-Vacuum Thermal Bleacher

The oil slurry enters an agitated vacuum reactor operated at 95°C to 110°C under 28 inches Hg deep vacuum. Thermal agitation strips trace moisture to <0.25% while maximizing adsorptive interaction between clay active sites and coloring bodies.

Operating Temp:95°C – 110°C
Vacuum Level:28" Hg Deep Vacuum
STAGE 04 Hermetic Leaf

Pressure Leaf & Security Polish Filtration

Bleached oil is pumped through vertical pressure leaf filters (VPLF) operating under 3.5 bar. Spent bleaching earth forms an impermeable cake and is vibrated dry. The crystal-clear oil passes through 5-micron security guard cartridges to ensure complete optical brilliance.

Filtration Media:VPLF + 5µ Polish
Colour Lovibond:Max 20Y + 2R (FSG)

Tertiary Processing · Catalytic Synthesis

Castor Oil Derivatives & Co-Products Manufacturing

Precision catalytic hydrogenation, alkaline hydrolysis, sulphuric acidulation, and spent lye vacuum distillation synthesizing Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO), Ricinoleic Acid, 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA), and Refined Glycerin.

ENGINEERING SCHEMATIC: DERIVATIVES SYNTHESIS & CO-PRODUCT RECOVERY
Catalytic Unit Hydrogen (H₂) / Acid Alkaline Caustic Commercial Output

📋 Reaction Stoichiometry & Operating Conditions

STAGE 01 Hydrogenation

Catalytic Hydrogenation to HCO Wax

1,000 kg of Refined FSG Castor Oil is reacted with 92 Nm³ of high-purity hydrogen gas under 3.0 to 5.0 bar pressure in the presence of 1.2 kg active supported Nickel catalyst at 140°C–180°C. Double bonds are selectively saturated, yielding 1,000 kg of hard white HCO wax.

H₂ Gas Consumption:92 Nm³ / MT Oil
Iodine Value Drop:82–90 → < 4.0 Units
Melting Point:84.0°C – 88.0°C
View Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO) Specs →
STAGE 02 Hydrolysis

Alkaline Hydrolysis

1,000 kg molten HCO is continuously fed into the hydrolysis reactor and reacted with 150 kg of 30% caustic soda (NaOH) solution. The triglyceride ester linkages are cleaved, producing sodium 12-hydroxystearate soap and releasing free glycerin in an aqueous medium.

NaOH (30%) Dosing:150 kg / MT HCO
Reaction Type:Triglyceride Hydrolysis
STAGE 03 Acidulation

Acidulation & 12-HSA Flaking

The soap is acidulated with 190 kg of 60% sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) and washed with 400 kg demineralized water. The mixture separates cleanly into an organic fatty acid layer (940 kg 12-HSA chilled and flaked on rotary drums) and an aqueous spent lye layer containing glycerin and sodium sulphate.

12-HSA Yield:940 kg (Losses: 19 kg)
Acid Value:Min. 175 mg KOH/g
Melting Point:Min. 74.0°C
View 12-Hydroxystearic Acid Specs →
STAGE 04 Co-Products

Spent Lye Evaporation & Salt Recovery

The spent lye aqueous phase (92 kg Glycerin + 250 kg Na₂SO₄ + 850 kg H₂O) is treated and concentrated in multi-effect evaporators. Centrifugal crystallization separates 250 kg of Sodium Sulphate Anhydrous (under development), while the concentrated glycerin is distilled to produce 69 kg of Refined Glycerin (under development).

Refined Glycerin:69 kg (99.5% Min)
Sodium Sulphate:250 kg (99.40% Min)
