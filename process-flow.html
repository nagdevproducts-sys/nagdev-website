Integrated Chemical Engineering

Castor Oil & Derivatives Process Flow

Our integrated Banaskantha facility operates automated Seed Processing, Solvent Extraction, Multi-Stage Vacuum Refining, and Catalytic Synthesis to produce premium industrial castor oils, high-protein organic meals, and precision 12-HSA & HCO derivatives.

Stage 1 · Oil Mill & Solvent Extraction Plant

Castor Oil Extraction Flow

From certified weighbridge receiving and silo cleaning through continuous mechanical expelling to normal hexane solvent extraction yielding Commercial Crude Oil and De-Oiled Cake.

01

Seed Intake & Pre-Cleaning

Incoming Gujarat castor seeds pass through computerized weighbridge sampling and aspirator screens. Broken seeds, stems, and dust are separated. Intact foreign husks are channeled to on-site biomass boilers.

Moisture: 5.5% – 6.5% · Purity: >99%
02

Mechanical Expeller Pressing

Cleaned seeds are conditioned and crushed in high-torque continuous screw expellers (120K / 70K capacity). 40% of the seed mass is extracted as pure, unrefined crude castor oil.

Primary Recovery: 40% Crude Oil
View Commercial Castor Oil Specs →
03

Solvent Extraction Plant (SEP)

Expeller cake containing 8% to 9% residual oil is transported to continuous counter-current extractors utilizing food-grade Normal Hexane. Hexane is stripped in desolventizer-toasters to yield high-quality de-oiled cake.

Hexane Recovery: >99.6% · Residual Oil: <1.0%

Stage 2 · Multi-Stage Chemical & Adsorption Refining

Castor Oil Refining Flow

Advanced degumming, neutralization, bleaching earth & activated carbon adsorption, and hermetic leaf filtration yielding high-purity First Special Grade (F.S.G / B.S.S), Pale Pressed (P.P.G), and British Pharmacopoeia (BP 93) grades.

01

Decantation & Foots Removal

Freshly expressed crude oil undergoes continuous centrifugal or gravity decantation. Heavy mucilage, plant gums, and seed particles ("foots") are recovered and recycled back to the expeller inlet to minimize losses.

Moisture Reduction · Solids <0.05%
02

Adsorptive Vacuum Bleaching

Commercial oil is heated under high vacuum (<20 mbar) and treated with acid-activated bentonite earth (Tonsil/Galleon), granular activated carbon, and Hyflow filter aid. This selectively adsorbs carotenoids, chlorophyll, oxidation peroxides, and traces of metal catalysts.

Bleaching Temp: 95°C – 105°C · Vacuum: 720 mmHg
03

Hermetic Leaf Filtration & Polishing

The bleached slurry is pumped through hermetic vertical pressure leaf filters followed by 1-micron cartridge polishers. All spent earth is separated, delivering crystal-clear, brilliant pale yellow oil compliant with international pharmaceutical and industrial standards.

Filtration Rating: 1.0 Micron Absolute

Stage 3 · Catalytic Hydrogenation, Saponification & Refining

Derivatives From Castor Oil

Precision synthesis pathways detailing the quantitative mass balance for manufacturing 12-HSA Flakes/Powder, Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO), and Pure Glycerin from 1000 kg Refined Castor Oil.

01

High-Pressure Hydrogenation (HCO)

1000 kg of Refined Castor Oil (FSG) is charged into high-pressure loop reactors with 92 Nm³ electrolytic Hydrogen gas in the presence of 1.2 kg active Nickel catalyst. Oleic and ricinoleic double bonds are completely saturated to yield 1000 kg HCO wax.

Reaction Temp: 140°C – 180°C · Pressure: 20 – 30 bar · I.V. < 4
View Hydrogenated Castor Oil Specs →
02

Saponification, Acidulation & Flaking (12-HSA)

The molten HCO is saponified with 150 kg caustic soda (30% NaOH), followed by mineral acidulation with 190 kg sulfuric acid (60% H₂SO₄) and 400 kg wash water. The purified fatty acid layer is solidified on chilling drums into 940 kg of high-purity 12-HSA flakes or powder.

Yield: 940 kg 12-HSA · Acid Value: >175 · Melting Pt: 74°C+
View 12-Hydroxy Stearic Acid Specs →
03

Glycerin Recovery & Sodium Sulphate Crystallization

The aqueous spent lye contains 92 kg dissolved glycerol and 250 kg sodium sulfate. Chemical coagulation and multiple-effect evaporation precipitate crystalline Sodium Sulphate Anhydrous (99.4% purity), while the concentrated crude sweetwater is high-vacuum distilled to produce 69 kg pure Industrial White / Technical Glycerin.

Yield: 69 kg Refined Glycerin + 250 kg Na₂SO₄ Anhydrous
