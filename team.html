Leadership & Management Team

Nagdev Products Private Limited combines deep generational roots in North Gujarat's agricultural castor economy with modern chemical process engineering and disciplined international B2B trade practices.

Connect with Management → About Nagdev Products

Generational Agriculture Meets Modern Oleochemistry

Our leadership team has been intimately involved with Banaskantha's castor farming communities for decades. Recognizing that Gujarat produces the majority of the world's finest castor seed while downstream chemical value addition frequently occurs overseas, Nagdev Products was conceived to bridge the gap between local farmgate seed intake and world-class chemical manufacturing.
