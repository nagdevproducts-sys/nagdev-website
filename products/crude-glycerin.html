Crude Glycerin Supplier India – Industrial Glycerin

Proprietary Grade: NDEVGLY™ CRUDE

Crude Glycerin (NDEVGLY™ CRUDE, CAS 56-81-5, glycerol 80% min) is industrial-grade unrefined vegetable glycerin co-produced during the high-pressure splitting of castor oil triglycerides. Containing natural glycerol, water, and residual organic matter, it serves as an economical carbon feedstock for anaerobic digestion, technical distillation, and animal feed manufacturing. Nagdev Products is a crude glycerin supplier India with bulk supply available on enquiry.

Industrial Scope

Target Applications & Uses

  • Commercial Glycerin Distillation: High glycerol concentration (80% min) makes it an economical feedstock for vacuum distillation into refined 99.5% glycerin.
  • Anaerobic Biogas Fermentation: Supplies an energy-dense, easily digestible carbon source to boost methane generation in industrial anaerobic digesters.
  • Dust Suppression & Binders: Formulated as a non-corrosive, hygroscopic dust control agent on unpaved haul roads, stockpiles, and foundry cores.

Commercial FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the chemical source of 80% crude castor glycerin? +

Obtained as sweet water byproduct during the high-pressure thermal splitting of castor oil, then concentrated via multi-effect evaporators.

What biogas digesters and oleochemical distillers procure 80% sweet water crude glycerin? +

Sold to commercial refiners for redistillation into CP glycerin, animal feed formulation, and anaerobic digesters for renewable methane generation.

In what bulk bulk packaging is crude glycerin supplied? +

Available on commercial enquiry in bulk road tankers, 20 MT flexitanks, and 1,000L composite IBC containers.
