Refined Glycerin Manufacturer Gujarat – 99.5% Pure

Proprietary Grade: NDEVGLY™ REF

Refined Glycerin (NDEVGLY™ REF, CAS 56-81-5) is a high-purity (99.5% minimum) pure vegetable glycerol produced from oleochemical splitting co-product streams. It offers exceptional humectant performance, optical clarity, and thermal stability for alkyd resins, polyurethane components, and chemical formulations. As a dedicated refined glycerin manufacturer Gujarat, Nagdev Products manufactures Refined Glycerin in regular commercial supply from our Gujarat plant.

Industrial Utility

Applications & Industry Uses

  • Alkyd Resins & Surface Coatings: Key polyhydric alcohol monomer for synthesizing short, medium, and long oil alkyd resins with outstanding gloss and flexibility.
  • Polyurethane Foam Chain Extender: Used as a cross-linking triol in rigid polyurethane insulation panels and flexible automotive seating foams.
  • Industrial Humectant & Plasticizer: Prevents moisture loss and brittleness in cellophane, cellulose sponges, paper products, and industrial glues.
  • De-icing & Hydraulic Formulations: Functions as a non-toxic freezing-point depressant and heat transfer fluid in specialized cooling and hydraulic systems.
Logistics & Cargo Handling

Packaging Formats & Bulk Loading

Export Packaging Options

Nagdev Products supplies industrial consignments in 200 kg tight-head steel drums, 1,000L composite IBC totes, dedicated road tankers, 21.5 MT flexitanks, and 20 to 24 MT ISO tank containers. 80 drums palletized (approx. 16.0–18.0 MT net) per 20ft container; 18–20 IBC totes (approx. 17.1–19.0 MT net) per 20ft container; flexitanks up to 21.5 MT. Consignments are dispatched directly from our Deodar plant to Mundra or Kandla seaport terminals.

Procurement FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How is 99.5% CP grade glycerin purified from castor split sweet-water? +

Sweet water from fat splitting is concentrated, triple vacuum-distilled, and carbon-bleached to yield anhydrous glycerol exceeding 99.5% purity.

What compendial limits define pure refined castor glycerin? +

Conforms to minimum 99.5% glycerol assay, specific gravity at 25°C of 1.2598 min, and heavy metals under 5 ppm.

What role does 99.5% pure glycerin play as a chain-extending triol in urethane casting? +

Its trifunctional alcohol backbone acts as a crosslinker and polymer building block in rigid polyurethanes, alkyd resins, and cellulose plasticization.

What logistics containers are used for refined glycerin dispatches? +

Supplied in 250 kg epoxy-lined steel drums, 1,250 kg food-grade IBC totes, and dedicated stainless steel ISO road tankers.

Which certificates of purity and analytical dossiers accompany refined glycerin lots? +

Every lot is backed by comprehensive COA verifying GC purity, moisture by Karl Fischer, and compendial traceability.
