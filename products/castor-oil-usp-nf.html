Proprietary Grade: NDEVOLEO™ USP

Refined castor oil produced from Nagdev's crushing and refining lines (refining, bleaching, filtration), supplied on enquiry. Each lot is tested against the requested United States Pharmacopeia–National Formulary (USP–NF) monograph and supplied with a lot Certificate of Analysis (COA). Regulated buyers qualify the supplier and individual lots per their specific internal requirements.

Target Uses

Applications & Industrial Scope

Supplied for pharmaceutical formulators requiring United States Pharmacopeia–National Formulary (USP–NF) compliance for technical compounding, excipient blending, and industrial preparations.

Supply & Logistics

Packaging Formats & Commercial Terms

Supplied on enquiry in standard export packaging: 200 kg steel drums, 950 kg IBC totes, or bulk ISO containers. Consignments include lot Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Technical Data Sheet. Third-party testing by SGS or Bureau Veritas available at buyer's cost. Explore our Bulk Castor Oil Supply and Export Hub.

Frequently Asked Questions

Castor Oil USP–NF – Common Questions

Which edition of the USP–NF monograph is referenced for batch testing? +

State the edition with your enquiry; testing is performed against the edition you request, and verified on the batch Certificate of Analysis.

How is batch analysis verified prior to dispatch? +

Each production lot is tested per compendial monograph parameters and dispatched with a detailed Certificate of Analysis (COA) for client qualification.

What standard bulk packaging formats are available? +

Castor Oil USP–NF is available in 200 kg steel or HDPE drums, 950 kg IBC totes, and bulk ISO tanks suited for export and domestic transport.
