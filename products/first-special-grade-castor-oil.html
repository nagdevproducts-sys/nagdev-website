Proprietary Grade: NDEVOLEO™ FSG

First Special Grade (FSG) Castor Oil (NDEVOLEO™ FSG, CAS 8001-79-4) is premium refined, bleached, and neutralized vegetable oil characterized by low free fatty acidity and light color. Featuring a pale yellow color, low free fatty acids, and high hydroxyl functionality, it serves urethane prepolymers, specialty coatings, and cosmetics. Nagdev Products supplies FSG castor oil in regular commercial supply from its operational plant at Deodar, Vav-Tharad district, North Gujarat.

Industrial Utility

Applications & Industry Uses

  • Urethane Elastomers & Adhesives: High purity and low moisture yield consistent NCO:OH stoichiometry in polyurethane adhesives, sealants, and elastomeric polymers.
  • Cosmetics & Personal Care: High optical clarity and emolliency make FSG the standard natural gloss agent for lipsticks, lip glosses, hair serums, and clear soaps.
  • Clear Greases & Lubricant Esters: Low acidity and pale Lovibond color prevent lacquer formation in high-temperature industrial greases and precision industrial oils.
  • Specialty Chemical Intermediates: Serves as the high-purity starting triglyceride for hydrogenation into HCO and subsequent splitting into 12-Hydroxystearic Acid.

Procurement FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does First Special Grade require BSS neutralization? +

FSG undergoes alkali neutralization, bleaching with activated earth, and filtration to remove free acidity and pigments, meeting British Standard Specifications.

Which optical absorbance and fatty acid limits govern refined FSG oil? +

Lovibond 5¼" cell: Max 20Y + 2R; FFA 1% max; moisture 0.25% max; hydroxyl 160–168 mg KOH/g.

How does FSG castor oil perform in polyurethane prepolymers? +

Consistent NCO:OH reaction kinetics and absence of moisture prevent pinhole bubbling in urethane potting compounds, clear adhesives, and coatings.

What export verification documents accompany FSG consignments? +

Export documents provided as required by the buyer: COA, TDS, SDS, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin.

Can overseas buyers request independent surveyor testing before shipment? +

Yes. Independent sampling and verification by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek can be conducted at Kandla or Mundra at the buyer's cost.
