First Special Grade (FSG) Castor Oil

NDEVOLEO™ FSG · CAS 8001-79-4 · HS 1515.30

First Special Grade Castor Oil (NDEVOLEO™ FSG, CAS 8001-79-4) is refined, bleached, and filtered castor oil conforming to British Standard Specifications (BSS). Featuring a pale yellow color, low free fatty acids, and high hydroxyl functionality, it serves urethane prepolymers, coatings, and lubricants. Nagdev Products produces FSG in regular commercial supply from our operational Deodar facility.

Key applications

Commercial and industrial applications for First Special Grade (FSG) Castor Oil supplied from our Deodar plant.

Urethane Elastomers & Adhesives

High purity, low acidity, and low moisture content ensure precise NCO:OH stoichiometry in structural polyurethanes, sealants, and potting resins.

Learn more →

Cosmetics & Personal Care

High optical clarity and natural emolliency make FSG an industry standard gloss enhancer in color cosmetics, creams, and lip formulations.

Learn more →

Clear Greases & Lubricant Esters

Low acidity and pale Lovibond colour prevent lacquer formation and oxidative thickening in formulated high-temperature bio-lubricants.

Learn more →

Specialty Chemical Intermediates

Serves as the high-purity starting triglyceride for hydrogenation to HCO, dehydration to DCO, and synthetic esterification.
