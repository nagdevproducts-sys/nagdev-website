Lithium 12-Hydroxystearate Specification & Status

Development Grade: NDEVSOAP™ LI12

Lithium 12-Hydroxystearate (NDEVSOAP™ LI12, CAS 7620-77-1) is pre-reacted soap powder made from 12-HSA and lithium hydroxide. It streamlines grease compounding by eliminating in-situ kettle saponification, forming high-drop-point matrices. Review our lithium 12-hydroxystearate specification below; grease makers are invited to enquire.

Development FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What processing benefit does pre-formed lithium soap offer grease kettles? +

Eliminates on-site handling of lithium hydroxide and water removal stages, reducing cooking cycle times by up to 40%.

What analytical limits govern the saponification completion of pre-formed lithium soap? +

Controlled lithium content of 2.2% to 2.5%, drop point over 200°C, and minimal free alkalinity below 0.2%.

How does pre-reacted soap ensure grease batch uniformity? +

Provides pre-crystallized soap fibers that dissolve predictably in mineral and synthetic base oils without saponification variance.
