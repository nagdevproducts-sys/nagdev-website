Frequently Asked Questions

Why is neutral anhydrous sodium sulphate used in powdered detergents?

Acts as an inert, non-corrosive, free-flowing bulking salt that regulates powder density and improves detergent solubility.

What purity and moisture standards govern technical sodium sulphate?

Minimum 99.0% Na2SO4 purity, moisture below 0.20%, water insolubles under 0.05%, and neutral pH in 5% aqueous solution.

Where is technical sodium sulphate applied outside detergents?

Used as a fining agent in container glass melting furnaces and as an electrolyte leveling salt in commercial textile dyeing.
