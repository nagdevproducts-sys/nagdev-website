Industrial Applications

Commercial and industrial applications for Cosmetic Grade Castor Oil supplied from our Deodar plant.

Lip Balms & Color Cosmetics

Natural gloss, rich pigment wetting, and high viscosity make it an ideal botanical binder for lipsticks, glosses, and mineral makeup.

Hair Care & Scalp Treatments

Rich ricinoleic acid content coats hair shafts, delivering natural shine and moisture retention in pomades, conditioning oils, and serums.

Nagdev Products provides specialized industrial packaging configured for domestic transport and ocean container export:

Quality Assurance & Lot Documentation

Every commercial delivery is backed by rigorous in-house analytical testing and operational quality control:

  • Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA): Issued per lot verifying specific batch parameters.
  • Technical Data Sheet (TDS): Technical parameter guidance and standard limits.
  • Safety Data Sheet (SDS): Globally Harmonized System (GHS) compliant handling documentation.
  • Operational Dispatch: Dispatched directly from our manufacturing plant in Deodar, Gujarat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What testing parameters are verified for cosmetic grade castor oil?

Testing is performed against the technical parameters agreed upon in the purchase specification, with batch COA issued per lot.

How is cosmetic grade castor oil packaged for personal care manufacturers?

Supplied in 200 kg steel or HDPE drums, 950 kg IBC totes, and bulk flexitanks tailored for cosmetic and personal care manufacturing.

What quality documents accompany each consignment?

Every order includes a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA), technical data sheet, standard safety data sheet (SDS), and dispatch records.
