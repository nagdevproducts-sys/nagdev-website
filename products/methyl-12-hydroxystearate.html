Frequently Asked Questions

How does methyl 12-HSA accelerate grease cooking compared to acid flakes?

Melts at 48–53°C (much lower than 12-HSA flakes), dissolving rapidly into base oils during lithium complex grease compounding.

Which melting range and hydroxyl values are planned for methyl 12-hydroxystearate?

Target parameters include melting point of 48°C to 53°C, hydroxyl value 165 to 180, and saponification value 170 to 185.

What non-grease uses utilize methyl 12-hydroxystearate?

Functions as an internal lubricant in vinyl extrusion, slip additive for polymers, and emollient wax in cosmetic sticks.
