Castor Oil

Refined Castor Oil (Pale Pressed Grade – P.P.G)

Proprietary Brand: NDEV-PPG

Pale Pressed Grade Castor Oil (proprietary brand NDEV-PPG, CAS #8001-79-4) is an extra-light refined vegetable oil produced from select high-maturity castor seeds through gentle cold-pressing followed by intensive bleaching earth filtration. It delivers superior clarity and minimal yellow undertones.

PPG is formulated primarily into color-critical applications such as clear lip glosses, decorative cosmetic emulsions, transparent varnishes, premium plasticizers, and high-clarity polyurethane sealants where yellowing cannot be tolerated.

Physical Form:Liquid
Typical Colour:Extra Pale / Nearly Water White

Industrial Utility

Typical Applications & Industry Uses

  • Cosmetics & Personal Care: Delivers pigment wetting, skin substantivity, and emolliency in stick, lotion, and emulsion formulations.
  • Lubricants & Greases: Formulated as a base stock, anti-wear additive, or thickener precursor delivering high film strength and shear stability.
  • Paints, Coatings & Inks: Utilized for film flexibility, non-yellowing gloss retention, and adhesion across protective epoxy and urethane systems.
  • Polymers, PVC & Rubber: Serves as an internal processing aid, secondary plasticizer, or lubricant improving melt flow and surface finish.
  • Polyurethanes & CASE: Reacts as a renewable bio-based polyol/chain extender providing elastomeric flexibility and water resistance.

Strategic Sourcing Advantage

Why Source from North Gujarat's Castor Belt

Nagdev Products is established in the agricultural core of the world's largest castor seed growing territory. Sourcing directly from Banaskantha and Vav-Tharad delivers distinct supply chain and technical advantages:

1. Immediate Seed Proximity

Direct farm-gate and APMC mandi sourcing across Banaskantha and Vav-Tharad ensures fresh crop intake without transit oxidation.

2. Natural Oil Content

North Gujarat castor seeds consistently exhibit superior 48–52% oil content and high ricinoleic fatty acid fractions (85% Min).

3. Supply Chain Consistency

Year-round local raw material buffer inventories safeguard uninterrupted processing and term supply contract fulfillments.

4. Integrated Value Chain

On-site seed expelling, chemical refining, and derivative synthesis eliminate trans-shipment handling and thermal degradation.
