Commercial Castor Oil

Proprietary Grade: NDEVOLEO™ COMM

Used as a raw material for manufacture of castor-oil derivatives and for industrial applications including lubricants, coatings, paints, adhesives, plasticizers, textile chemicals and printing inks.

Typical Colour:Pale Yellow to Yellow
Feedstock:Castor Seed (Ricinus communis)
