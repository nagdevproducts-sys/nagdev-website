Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO)

Proprietary Grade: NDEVPOL™ DCO

Used as a semi-drying/drying oil in paints, varnishes, alkyd resins, printing inks, primers, caulks, sealants and protective coatings. Its increased unsaturation (largely conjugated diene) enables oxidative drying and film formation.

Physical Form:Liquid
Typical Colour:Pale Yellow to Amber
Feedstock:Refined Castor Oil
Shelf Life:18-24 months
Standard Export Packaging:Bulk in ISO Tank / Flexi Tank • 200 kg HDPE / MS Drums • 1,000 kg IBC • Bulk in Vessel for large-volume shipments (20-22 MT) • Or as recommended / specified by client
