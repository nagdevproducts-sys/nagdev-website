Frequently Asked Questions

Why is zinc undecylenate formulated into topical antifungal powders?

Exhibits proven fungistatic action against athlete's foot and dermatophyte fungi while providing soothing astringent skin protection.

Which analytical assay tests confirm compendial monograph compliance for zinc undecylenate powder?

Monograph standards mandate 12.0–14.0% zinc content, melting point 115–120°C, and loss on drying under 1.2%.

How is zinc undecylenate synthesized from castor oil?

Undecylenic acid (obtained from high-temperature vacuum pyrolysis of castor oil) is reacted with high-purity zinc oxide.
