Gateway Seaports

Western India Seaport Logistics: Kandla & Mundra

Our manufacturing plant in Sardarpura, Taluka Deodar is strategically positioned along the western Gujarat logistics corridor, minimizing domestic transit time and freight risk:

Deendayal Port (Kandla) · ~220 km

Kandla Port is India's leading seaport by liquid cargo volume. With dedicated vegetable oil discharge berths, heavy-duty pumping lines, and nearby licensed tank storage terminals, Kandla is our primary hub for large-scale flexitank dispatches and bulk tanker loading.

Bulk Liquid Freight Specialist

Mundra Port (APSEZ) · ~260 km

Mundra is India's major private commercial container port. Offering state-of-the-art container freight stations (CFS), rapid automated customs sealing, and extensive weekly liner loops from Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, and Hapag-Lloyd, Mundra is our primary hub for FCL drum and bag shipments.

Scheduled Containerized FCL Specialist

Trucking transit from our Deodar plant to port gates typically takes less than 6 hours. This proximity prevents transit exposure and enables immediate container gate-in ahead of scheduled ocean vessel cut-offs.

Trade Compliance

Complete Export Documentation & Quality Certification

To ensure problem-free customs release and banking compliance, Nagdev Products provides an authenticated international trade document portfolio with every export order:

Clean On Board Bill of Lading (BL)

Ocean carrier bill of lading with clear freight markings, container and seal numbers, and consigned according to buyer’s Letter of Credit (LC) terms.

Certificate of Origin (COO)

Government-certified preferential or non-preferential Certificate of Origin issued by authorized Indian Chambers of Commerce or Export Inspection Agency.

Factory Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Lot-specific chemical laboratory report recording actual analytical results (FFA, Lovibond color, hydroxyl value, moisture, melting point).

Third-Party Surveyor Inspection

Independent pre-shipment sampling, testing, and container sealing by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek executed at buyer’s request/cost.

Export FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions: Exporting Castor Oil from India

Which gateway ports are utilized for export shipments from India?

Our export consignments are routed through Kandla Seaport (~220 km) and Mundra Seaport (~260 km) in Gujarat. Both ports provide frequent weekly liner sailings with direct container loops to European, North American, East Asian, and Middle Eastern hub ports.

What export documentation is provided with every international consignment?

Every shipment is supported by export documents as required by the buyer: Ocean Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (COO), factory Certificate of Analysis (COA), Technical Data Sheet (TDS), Safety Data Sheet (SDS), and phytosanitary certificate for castor cake/meal shipments where the importing country requires it.

Can overseas buyers request independent pre-shipment quality inspection?

Yes. Overseas buyers may appoint accredited international testing houses (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) to conduct independent sampling, laboratory verification, and container stuffing supervision at buyer's cost prior to port dispatch.

Which Incoterms are supported for international castor oil trade?

We quote under standard ICC Incoterms 2020: FOB (Kandla / Mundra), CFR destination port, CIF destination port, and DAP for select overland or direct-delivered programs.

What are the primary export packaging options for overseas transit?

We supply liquid oils in 20–24 MT ISO tank containers, 21.5 MT flexitanks, 1,000 L composite IBC totes, and 200 kg new steel drums (80 drums / 16 MT per 20ft container). Solid derivatives (HCO wax and 12-HSA) are supplied in 25 kg palletized bags (20 MT per 20ft container).
