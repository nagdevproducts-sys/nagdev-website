Global Export Leadership

Castor Oil Export from India — Nagdev Products

India supplies over 85% of total international castor oil demand, making it the strategic global backbone for renewable oleochemical manufacturing. Nagdev Products Private Limited serves industrial buyers, specialty chemical compounders, and overseas distributors as an integrated Indian manufacturer and exporter.

Headquartered in Gandhinagar and operating a dedicated 50 TPD crushing and processing plant in Sardarpura, Deodar (North Gujarat), our operations connect direct farm-gate seed origination with major commercial gateway ports. Our export division manages containerized ocean freight, international banking compliance, and rigorous quality control to ensure dependable global shipments.

Global Reach

Export Markets & Countries Served

Nagdev Products exports to industrial customers across major global trade corridors, adapting packaging and compliance documentation to regional regulatory standards:

United States & North America

Regular dispatches to Houston, New York/New Jersey, Long Beach, and Savannah. Focused on TSCA inventory compliance, HTSUS classification, and technical HCO and DCO for lubricant and urethane manufacture.

Europe (Germany, Netherlands, UK)

Container routes to Rotterdam, Hamburg, Antwerp, and Genoa. Strict compliance with EU regulatory standards, low-color First Special Grade (FSG) for bio-polyurethanes, and GHS-aligned safety sheets.

China & East Asia (Japan, Korea)

High-volume flexitank supply to Shanghai, Ningbo, Qingdao, Busan, and Yokohama. Serving chemical splitting, nylon precursors, high-temp greases, and industrial coatings.

Southeast Asia & Middle East

Direct sea lanes to Singapore, Port Klang, Bangkok, Dubai (Jebel Ali), and Dammam for chemical blending, industrial grease synthesis, and agrochemical formulations.

Logistics & Stowage

Export Packing & Container Loading

Ocean containerization requires high packaging integrity to withstand sea transit, temperature fluctuations, and intermodal handling. We provide standard export stowage configurations:

  • 200-Litre New Steel Drums: 180 kg net weight per drum. Approximately 80 drums per 20-foot container (approx. 14.4 to 16.0 MT net cargo weight). Loaded with or without wooden/plastic heat-treated pallets (ISPM-15 compliant).
  • Flexi-Bags & Flexitanks: 950 kg intermediate flexi-totes and 20 to 22 MT bulk food-grade polyethylene flexitanks fitted in 20ft dry containers. Cost-effective for high-volume liquid consumption.
  • Dedicated ISO Tanks: 20–24 MT payload in stainless steel ISO tank containers for closed-loop industrial unloading systems.
  • 25 kg Moisture-Barrier Bags: For HCO wax flakes and organic castor cake, packed 16 to 20 MT per 20ft container, palletized and shrink-wrapped.
Document Dossier

Export Documentation Provided

Each export consignment is accompanied by a complete, authenticated international trade dossier:

  • Certificate of Analysis (COA): Lot-specific lab report covering full AOCS parameters.
  • Technical Data Sheet (TDS): Comprehensive technical specifications and properties.
  • Safety Data Sheet (SDS): 16-section GHS-compliant material safety documentation.
  • FSSAI Export Certificate: Government verification of food safety and processing registration.
  • Certificate of Origin (COO): Legal chamber-attested origin certification for customs duty assessment.
  • Phytosanitary Certificate: Plant health certificate for castor cake agricultural shipments.
  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List: Authenticated commercial documentation detailing weights, batch numbers, and tariff codes.
  • Clean On Board Ocean Bill of Lading (BL): Conforming strictly to buyer's documentary instructions.
Commercial Terms

Incoterms & Payment

Nagdev Products contracts under standard ICC Incoterms 2020 rules to ensure contractual clarity:

  • FOB Mundra Port (INMUN): India's major commercial private container port in Gujarat, located ~260 km from our plant. Fast gate-in and extensive weekly vessel connections.
  • FOB Nhava Sheva (JNPT, INNSA): Primary container gateway in Maharashtra for specialized global trade lanes.
  • CIF / CFR: Cost, Insurance and Freight delivered to buyer's named overseas port of destination.
  • Ex-Works (EXW): Direct collection from our Deodar crushing plant for domestic exporters.

Payment Terms: Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at sight) from prime international banks, or Telegraphic Transfer (TT) with 30% advance deposit and balance against draft BL copies.

Trade FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ports do you export from?

Primarily Mundra Port (INMUN) and Nhava Sheva (JNPT, INNSA), Gujarat and Maharashtra.

What export documents do you provide?

COA, TDS, SDS, FSSAI Export Certificate, Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading.

Which Incoterms do you offer?

FOB Mundra, FOB Nhava Sheva, CIF, and Ex-Works. We can discuss other Incoterms for established buyers.

What is the minimum order quantity for export?

Typically 1 × 20-foot FCL (approx. 16 MT in drums or 20 MT in flexi-bags), but we can discuss smaller LCL shipments.

Do you have FSSAI registration for export?

Yes, Nagdev Products Private Limited holds FSSAI registration and can provide export health certificates.

Can you match a competitor's COA specification?

Yes, we can produce to buyer-specified parameters within our grades' technical range, confirmed by advance COA before shipment.
