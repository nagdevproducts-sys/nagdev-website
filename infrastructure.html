✦ Integrated Crushing & Downstream Oleochemical Facility

Plant Infrastructure & Engineering Specifications

Integrated castor processing facility featuring mechanical pressing, semi-continuous chemical refining, batch derivative reactors, and in-house quality control in Deodar, North Gujarat.

Our Plant in Operation → Explore Interactive Process Flow
Primary Processing

1. Seed Cleaning, Preparation & Mechanical Pressing

Our integrated plant processes whole castor seeds sourced from North Gujarat growers.

Seed Cleaning & Preparation Section

Mechanical cleaning equipment removes foreign matter, sand, dust, and impurities to prepare whole castor seeds for uniform expelling.

Mechanical Pressing (Expellers)

Continuous mechanical screw expellers process castor seeds for first-stage mechanical oil expression, producing unrefined crude castor oil and generating expeller castor cake for organic manure conditioning.

2. Semi-Continuous Refining & Expeller Cake Conditioning

Primary crude castor oil from the mechanical expellers is neutralised, bleached under high vacuum, and deodorised to produce export-grade First Special Grade (FSG) oil. The co-produced expeller cake is naturally conditioned and packed as organic manure.

Expeller Cake Conditioning

Mechanical screw press cake containing typically 8–10% residual oil and 4.0–4.5% organic nitrogen is cooled and packed in heavy-duty HDPE bags as an all-natural organic soil amendment and conditioner.

Chemical & Bleaching Refinery

Semi-continuous neutralizer-bleacher line with high-vacuum activated bleaching earth reactors and sparkler filter presses, producing First Special Grade (FSG) and Pale Pressed Grade (PPG) oils.

Derivatives Manufacturing

3. Batch Pressure Reactors for Castor Derivatives

Our oleochemical derivative section is equipped with two batch pressure reactors dedicated to the synthesis of:

  • Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO / Castor Wax): High-melting-point wax manufactured via pressure hydrogenation.
  • Ricinoleic Acid: High-purity hydroxy fatty acid synthesized via controlled chemical splitting.
  • 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA): Key chemical intermediate produced for industrial grease thickeners and polymers.

Note on derivative operations: Batch pressure reactors for derivatives operate with precision batch scheduling to meet customer volume requirements.

Storage & Quality Control

4. Storage Facilities & In-House Testing Laboratory

Storage Infrastructure

Dedicated storage tanks for crude and refined castor oils, alongside covered warehouse storage facilities for raw castor seed intake and processed castor cake (expeller cake).

In-House QC Laboratory

On-site quality control laboratory equipped to test and verify standard oil parameters: Acid Value (AV), Hydroxyl Value (OHV), Iodine Value (IV), Moisture content, and Colour.
