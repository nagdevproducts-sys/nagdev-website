## India's Maritime Export Gateways for Oleochemicals

India accounts for the vast majority of the world's exported castor oil, supplying vital chemical feedstocks to industrial manufacturers across Europe, the Americas, East Asia, and the Middle East. For international procurement managers, understanding the maritime supply chain—from processing plant gates to container loading berths—is vital to ensuring on-time delivery and product integrity. In this practical export logistics guide, we evaluate the protocols, container choices, and regulatory documentation required when exporting castor oil from India ports.

Geographically, the vast majority of Indian castor seed crushing and refining occurs within Gujarat. Consequently, maritime export movements are concentrated in Gujarat's premier deep-water ports situated along the Gulf of Kutch: Mundra Port and Deendayal Port (Kandla). Operating our crushing and refining facility in Deodar, North Gujarat, Nagdev Products maintains a direct freight corridor to both maritime gateways.

## Port Infrastructure: Exporting Castor Oil from India Ports

Evaluating the logistical infrastructure of Mundra and Kandla highlights specific capabilities for bulk and containerized shipping:

- **Mundra Port (APSEZ)**: India's largest private commercial port features state-of-the-art container terminals, deep-water container berths, dedicated railway freight corridors, and specialized tank farms. Mundra provides frequent, direct container liner sailings to Rotterdam, Antwerp, Hamburg, Felixstowe, New York, Houston, Shanghai, Busan, and Yokohama.
- **Deendayal Port (Kandla)**: A major public port authority with extensive liquid bulk terminal facilities, specialized vegetable oil jetties, and dedicated pipeline manifolds. Kandla handles large parcel tankers for bulk liquid vegetable oil exports alongside containerized cargo.
- **Domestic Transit Corridor from Deodar**: Situated at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Post Ravel, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330, our plant is located approximately 240 to 280 kilometers from these ports. Dedicated freight carriers transport sealed container loads to port container freight stations (CFS) via direct highway corridors, minimizing pre-shipment delays.

## Ocean Packaging Configurations for International Buyers

Selecting the appropriate export packaging protects refined castor oil and its derivatives against moisture ingress, contamination, and thermal oxidation during multi-week ocean voyages:

1. **Epoxy-Lined Steel Drums (200 kg net / 80 drums per 20ft FCL)**: Internal baked epoxy linings isolate refined oils from raw steel surfaces. Drums are secured onto fumigated wooden or plastic pallets and secured with heavy-duty lashing inside twenty-foot ocean containers.
2. **Intermediate Bulk Containers (1,000 L IBC totes / 18 to 20 IBCs per 20ft FCL)**: High-density polyethylene composite bottles in tubular steel cages provide efficient mechanical handling and forklift maneuverability at destination warehouses.
3. **Multi-Layer Flexitanks (20 to 21.5 MT in 20ft Dry Box)**: sanitary, high-barrier polyethylene flexitanks convert standard dry ocean containers into bulk liquid carriers, delivering the most economical freight rates for large-volume industrial consumers.
4. **Dedicated Stainless Steel ISO Tanks (20 to 25 MT)**: Insulated, temperature-controllable ISO tanks equipped with top and bottom discharge valves for high-purity pharmaceutical and technical excipient buyers.

## Export Documentation Dossier and Regulatory Clearance

Export consignments managed by Nagdev Products are accompanied by complete, legally complete shipping dossiers:

- **Commercial Invoice and Packing List**: Detailing exact batch lot numbers, gross and net weights, HS codes, and container numbers.
- **Clean on Board Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L)**: Issued by premier container shipping lines.
- **Certificate of Origin (Preferential / Non-Preferential)**: Certified by authorized chambers of commerce to facilitate duty-free or concessional tariff clearance under international trade agreements.
- **Lot-Specific Certificate of Analysis (COA)**: Certified by our quality laboratory, detailing physical-chemical constants matching contract specifications.
- **Safety Data Sheet (SDS) **: Formatted to international GHS standards for European and American customs authorities.
