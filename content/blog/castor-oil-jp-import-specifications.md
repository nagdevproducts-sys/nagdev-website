## Production Process and Quality Verification in Deodar

Nagdev Products manufactures Castor Oil JP at our integrated facility situated at Survey No. 301 P-2, Jada-Kotarwada Road, Village Sardarpura, Post Ravel, Taluka Deodar, District Vav-Tharad, Gujarat 385330. With an authorized 50 TPD seed crushing capacity, our production workflow follows strict quality engineering procedures:

- **Seed Sorting and Destoning**: Selected castor seeds from North Gujarat agricultural mandis are cleaned using vibrating screen sieves, air classifiers, and magnetic separators to remove field dust, hulls, and metallic contamination.
- **Mechanical Expelling**: Cleaned seeds are crushed mechanically in heavy-duty expellers without chemical extraction or chemical aids, isolating the native vegetable triglyceride.
- **Alkali alkali refining**: The raw oil undergoes controlled caustic refining with aqueous sodium hydroxide to refine with alkali free fatty acids, precipitating them as soapstock.
- **Demineralized Water Washing**: The refined liquid is washed with warm demineralized water to remove all traces of dissolved soaps and polar impurities.
- **Continuous Vacuum Dehydration**: Washed oil enters a vacuum drying chamber to flash off residual water molecules, keeping moisture well within compendial limits.
- **Adsorbent Earth Bleaching**: The dry neutral oil is treated with activated bleaching earth under vacuum to adsorb coloring bodies and trace impurities.
- **Fine Polishing Filtration**: The bleached liquid passes through enclosed polishing filter presses into clean storage tanks for full laboratory monograph analysis.

Key analytical parameters evaluated for Castor Oil JP include:

- **Clarity and Color of Solution**: Testing ensures the oil forms a clear, unclouded liquid that meets defined optical transmission limits, verifying absence of suspended matter or moisture haze.
- **Specific Optical Rotation**: Dextrorotatory optical activity within monograph positive limits confirms authentic botanical provenance and rules out foreign seed oil adulteration.
