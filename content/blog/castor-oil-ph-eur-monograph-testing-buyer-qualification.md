In the European Pharmacopoeia, Castor Oil is governed by Monograph 0051 (covering both *Castor Oil, Virgin* and *Castor Oil, Refined*). ## Analytical Methodology: Castor Oil Ph Eur Monograph Testing

Procurement auditors reviewing batch Certificates of Analysis against Monograph 0051 criteria evaluate several core analytical determinations:

- **Chromatographic Fatty Acid Profile (Ph. Eur. 2.4.22)**: Monograph 0051 requires gas chromatography with flame ionization detection (GC-FID) of fatty acid methyl esters (FAME). Ricinoleic acid must fall within specified percentage boundaries (monograph percentage limits). Strict percentage ceilings are enforced on palmitic (monograph limits), stearic (monograph limits), oleic (monograph limits), linoleic (monograph limits), linolenic (monograph limits), and dihydroxystearic acid (monograph limits). This comprehensive profile proves authentic botanical identity and precludes adulteration with foreign vegetable oils.
- **Specific Optical Rotation (Ph. Eur. 2.2.7)**: Measured in a calibrated polarimeter at 20°C, authentic castor oil exhibits dextrorotatory optical activity within monograph positive limits. This optical activity arises from the chiral carbon center at C-12 of the ricinoleic acid molecule, providing definitive proof of botanical origin.
- **Light Absorption in the Ultraviolet Spectrum (Ph. Eur. 2.2.25)**: Spectrophotometric examination measures light absorbance in the ultraviolet spectrum. Low absorbance values verify the absence of conjugated dienes and confirm that the oil was not exposed to excessive heat during pressing or refining.
- **Acid Value and Peroxide Value**: Ph. Eur. enforces strict limits on free acidity (acid value max 2.0 mg KOH/g) and oxidative status (peroxide value max 5.0 to 10.0 meq/kg), ensuring chemical freshness and ester integrity.
- **Relative Density and Refractive Index**: Precise measurements confirm standard physical density (0.952 to 0.965 at 20°C) and optical refraction (1.477 to 1.481 at 20°C).
