## Production Process and Quality Verification in Deodar

Nagdev Products manufactures Castor Oil Ph. With an authorized 50 TPD seed crushing capacity, our production workflow follows strict quality engineering protocols:

- **Seed Cleaning and Sizing**: Sound castor seeds sourced from agricultural mandis across North Gujarat are processed through mechanical air separators, vibrating screens, and magnetic grids to strip out all non-seed matter.
- **Mechanical Screw Expelling**: Seed crushing is carried out mechanically without the addition of chemical solvents or extraction chemicals, isolating the native vegetable triglyceride.
- **Chemical alkali refining**: Expelled crude oil undergoes controlled caustic refining with aqueous sodium hydroxide to refine with alkali free fatty acids into insoluble soapstock.
- **Water Washing**: Neutral oil is washed repeatedly with warm demineralized water to remove all traces of alkaline soaps and water-soluble impurities.
- **Vacuum Dehydration**: The washed oil enters a continuous vacuum dehydration unit to eliminate moisture below compendial limits.
- **Adsorbent Earth Bleaching**: The dry neutral oil is treated with activated bleaching earth under vacuum to adsorb colored bodies and trace oxidation precursors.
- **Polishing Filtration**: The bleached liquid is filtered through enclosed plate-and-frame polishing presses into dedicated staging tanks for batch analytical clearance.

## Monograph 0051 Analytical Specifications & Purity Profile

Key analytical determinations include:

- **Fatty Acid Composition by GC-FID**: Monograph 0051 enforces strict chromatographic limits on fatty acid methyl esters. Ricinoleic acid must fall within specified percentage boundaries (monograph percentage limits), while palmitic, stearic, oleic, linoleic, linolenic, and dihydroxystearic acids must remain strictly within their individual thresholds.
- **Specific Optical Rotation**: Dextrorotatory optical activity within monograph positive limits confirms authentic botanical provenance and rules out foreign seed oil adulteration.
- **Absorbance in the Ultraviolet Range**: Spectrophotometric measurement in the ultraviolet spectrum ensures low conjugated diene content and confirms that the oil has not undergone excessive thermal exposure during refining.
- **Acid Value and Peroxide Value**: Stringent limits verify low free acidity and pronounced oxidative freshness.
- **Relative Density and Refractive Index**: Confirm standard physical characteristics of pure ricinoleic triglycerides.
