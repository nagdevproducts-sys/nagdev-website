## What Defines Castor Oil BP under British Compendial Standards?

In pharmaceutical manufacturing, chemical compounding, and high-grade industrial synthesis, raw material specifications must adhere to rigorous compendial texts. Castor Oil BP designates refined vegetable oil that fulfills the analytical monograph published by the British Pharmacopoeia Commission. Widely utilized across Commonwealth markets and international regulatory jurisdictions, the BP compendium outlines exacting qualitative identification thresholds and quantitative physical-chemical limits. This castor oil BP monograph buyer guide provides quality managers, regulatory affairs executives, and procurement specialists with the technical foundation needed to evaluate and approve bulk supply lots.

The British Pharmacopoeia establishes that castor oil is the fatty oil obtained from the seeds of *Ricinus communis*. Unlike conventional commercial oils intended for general industrial utility, Castor Oil BP demands strict verification of chemical authenticity. Testing must establish not only the presence of the characteristic ricinoleic triglyceride but also confirm the absence of foreign vegetable oils, residual chemical extraction solvents, and rancid degradation products. ## Critical Monograph Parameters and Laboratory Analysis

Technical auditors reviewing lot documentation for Castor Oil BP focus on specific analytical parameters that substantiate botanical identity and purity:

{{spec_table}}

- **Specific Optical Rotation**: Because of the asymmetric carbon atom at C-12 of the ricinoleic acid chain, pure castor oil rotates plane-polarized light in a clockwise direction. BP criteria require specific optical rotation within monograph positive limits. This metric serves as an infallible fingerprint distinguishing authentic castor oil from non-rotational adulterant oils.
- **Acid Value**: Monograph standards enforce tight acid value limits to prove minimal hydrolytic degradation of the triglyceride molecules during expelling and storage.
- **Refractive Index and Relative Density**: Measured precisely at standard temperature using calibrated refractometers and pycnometers, confirming molecular density and hydroxyl concentration.
- **Absorbance in the Ultraviolet Spectrum**: Monograph testing checks UV absorbance at designated wavelengths to verify the absence of conjugated dienes and thermal oxidation artifacts.
- **soap formation and Iodine Values**: Ensure that the ester bond concentration and degree of fatty acid unsaturation conform strictly to compendial norms.
