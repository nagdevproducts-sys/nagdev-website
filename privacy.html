Privacy Policy & Cookie Information

Information on data handling, Google Analytics 4 measurement, and consent controls.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Organization & Site Ownership

Nagdev Products Private Limited (CIN: U15400GJ2019PTC110518) operates this website (https://www.nagdevproducts.com). We are an integrated Indian castor oil and chemical derivatives manufacturer and exporter based in Gujarat, India.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-HL3JQ3ZQKF) to measure aggregated site usage and understand how buyers browse our product specifications and technical resources.

  • No advertising cookies: We do not deploy advertising or marketing tracking. Google signals and ad personalisation signals are permanently disabled.
  • Consent Mode v2: Visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland are presented with a consent banner where analytics storage defaults to denied until explicit permission is given.
  • Visitor Choice: Anyone can review or change their cookie preferences at any time by clicking the link located in the footer of every page.

3. Commercial Inquiries & Form Data

When you submit an official inquiry, sample request, or technical query through our forms:

  • Data collected (including your name, company name, corporate email address, phone/WhatsApp number, country, and product requirement details) is utilized exclusively to respond to your commercial or technical inquiries and deliver requested documentation (COA, TDS, SDS, pricing).
  • We never sell, rent, lease, or share inquiry data with third-party marketing brokers or advertising networks.

Enquiry forms on this site are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-processing service. We use these details only to respond to your enquiry.
