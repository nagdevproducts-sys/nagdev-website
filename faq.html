Products & Specifications

What is the primary fatty acid component of castor oil? +

Ricinoleic acid (12-hydroxy-9-octadecenoic acid) makes up approximately 88% to 90% of the total fatty acid composition in natural castor oil, conferring unique hydroxyl functionality and extreme natural viscosity.

What is the difference between First Special Grade (FSG) and Commercial Grade Castor Oil? +

FSG is neutralized, bleached, and dried to achieve an acid value under 2.0 mg KOH/g and pale color (Lovibond ≤ 20Y + 2.0R), whereas Commercial Grade is unrefined filtered expeller oil with higher natural acidity (≤ 4.0–6.0 mg KOH/g) suited for industrial lubricants and cutting oils.

What is the melting point of Hydrogenated Castor Oil (Castor Wax)? +

Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO) has a melting point of 85.0°C to 88.0°C and an iodine value strictly under 3.0 g I₂/100g, forming a hard, brittle, microcrystalline wax.

How does 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA) differ chemically from HCO? +

HCO is a neutral triglyceride containing bound glycerol, while 12-HSA is a pure monocarboxylic free fatty acid (acid value 175–182 mg KOH/g, melting point 75°C–78°C) produced by splitting HCO.

Can custom parameter specifications be accommodated for specialty buyers? +

Yes. Within our processing lines, parameters such as lower moisture limits (<0.08% for polyurethanes), extra-low acid values, and customized color profiles can be tailored for forward contract volume commitments.

Quality Assurance & Testing

What quality management system governs Nagdev Products' facility? +

Our plant will operate under quality systems aligned to ISO 9001; formal certification is planned after commercial commissioning in 2027.

What analytical test methods are used in your in-house laboratory? +

We test all parameters using standardized compendial methods: AOCS (American Oil Chemists' Society), ASTM International, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), and British Pharmacopoeia (BP).

Does every consignment ship with a Certificate of Analysis (COA)? +

Yes. Every single production batch is analyzed and accompanied by an authentic lot-traceable Certificate of Analysis detailing tested values against guaranteed technical specifications.

Can buyers arrange third-party pre-shipment inspections? +

Yes. We welcome international inspection surveyors (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) to conduct independent sampling, batch analysis, and container sealing at our facility prior to dispatch.

Are Nagdev Products' products REACH compliant for export to the European Union? +

We are preparing complete technical dossiers and Only Representative (OR) structures to ensure seamless REACH compliance for EU industrial importers upon commercial 2027 launch.

Shipping & Export Documentation

What export packaging formats are available for liquid castor oils? +

We provide 200 kg / 225 kg new tight-head steel and HDPE drums, 1,000L composite IBC totes, and bulk shipments in 20–22 MT flexitanks or up to 24 MT ISO tank containers.

How are solid flakes and powders (12-HSA, HCO) packaged? +

Packed in 25 kg multi-wall kraft paper bags with an inner moisture-barrier polyethylene liner, stacked on fumigated export pallets and shrink-wrapped, or in 1,000 kg bulk jumbo super sacks.

What ports in India are used for export loading? +

Consignments are loaded out of Mundra Port (INMUN) or Kandla Port (INIXY) in Gujarat, well connected to Kandla and Mundra ports via direct highway corridors.

What standard trade documents accompany every export shipment? +

Each consignment includes Commercial Invoice, Packing List with container seal numbers, Clean on Board Ocean Bill of Lading, Certificate of Analysis (COA), Chamber Certificate of Origin, and GHS-compliant Safety Data Sheet (SDS).

What are the HS tariff codes for your primary product lines? +

Castor oils are classified under HS 1515.30, Hydrogenated Castor Oil under HS 1516.20, 12-HSA under HS 2918.19, De-Oiled Cake under HS 2306.90, and Sebacic Acid under HS 2917.13 (indicative classification; final HS code confirmed at export).
